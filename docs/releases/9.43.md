# SirisOS 9.43

- **Voice works after a pause.** Starting a voice chat a few minutes after the last one could answer "I didn't catch that". After a reply had played, or after a while idle, iPhone left the microphone's audio context interrupted, so the next recording was silence. SirisOS now resumes the context, or opens a fresh one if it was closed, before recording.
- **A clear hint when nothing is heard.** If a recording picked up nothing at all, SirisOS resets the microphone and says "The microphone didn't pick anything up. Tap to try again" instead of sending silence to SirisAI.

Works with any SirisAI. SirisAI 6.7 adds the Jarvis voice and replies without code.
