# SirisOS 4.7

**Smart home: devices you can see and move, and the house as it really is.**

- **Small models, not dots.** Every device is drawn as a little model of what it is: the gateway, the switch, access points, phones, tablets, watches, laptops, the gaming PC, servers and the NAS, the PlayStation, the Apple TV and TVs, HomePods and Echos, hubs, plugs, lights, the AC, vacuums, cameras, sensors and the fridge. The type comes from the device's name ("HomePod Mini - Office" is a speaker), or from the Home Assistant control's kind. Offline devices are grey.
- **Fewer things everywhere.** Devices within about 2 m of each other on a floor become one group, with a count on it and up to three of its models. Tap a group to see what's in it. The gateway, the switch and the access points always stay on their own, because the links run from them.
- **Move anything to where it really is.** Tap a device or a group, then drag it across its floor. A group moves together. To pull one device out of a group, pick it from the group's list and drag it. A device lands in the room you drop it in, and its exact spot is saved. The room list still moves a device to another floor, or hides it.
- **The house, corrected:**
  - **The stairs:** they're in their own walled corridor along the right-hand wall (seen from the street), stacked the whole way up and further back from the front. The roof no longer has a flight of its own.
  - **First floor:** the main bedroom is at the front, over the office. The spare bedroom is at the back, over the garage.
  - **Second floor:** it's the other way round, as in Brad's photo. The balcony faces the street, then the lounge (the TV is on the stair corridor's wall and the sofa faces it), dining, and the kitchen at the back.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Saved rooms carry over. Devices placed in the old "Entry & stairs" or "Hall & stairs" rooms now sit in Entry or Hallway.
