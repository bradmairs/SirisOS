# SirisOS 1.1

- **SirisLearn joins the hub** ([ADR 112](../adr/112-sirislearn.md)): a SirisLearn tile and a Learning widget with your streak, XP today against the daily goal, level, active courses, the next lesson and newly unlocked awards
- SirisLearn courses and lessons show up in ⌘K search
- The inbox reminds you when your learning streak ends at midnight and today's quiz isn't done, and opens the next lesson
- New settings: `SIRISLEARN_URL`, `SIRISLEARN_PUBLIC_URL`, `SIRISLEARN_HUB_KEY`
