# SirisOS 1.0

The first numbered release of the SirisOS hub: one Liquid Glass home screen
(an installable PWA) for every Siris app, with SirisAI built in as the
assistant and Second Brain as the notes layer.

## Highlights

- **Hub**: every Siris app as a live tile with health, plus its data as Home widgets ([ADR 106](../adr/106-sirisos-hub.md)).
- **Daily brief**: opens by itself until 9 am with your day, news from your Second Brain interests and what needs a look ([ADR 108](../adr/108-daily-brief.md)).
- **Search everything** (⌘K): apps, widgets, links, Second Brain, Siris chats, APD PM, the Archive, the Reviewer and the engineering library ([ADR 109](../adr/109-search-everything.md)).
- **SirisAI integration**: one inbox for alerts and approvals, protocols with undo, cameras, car and power on Home; SirisAI can use the standards library, calculators and SirisHydro ([ADR 110](../adr/110-sirisai-integration.md)).
- **Career**: the path to CPEng, NER and Victorian registration, with Stage 2 competencies, goals, next steps and CPD imported from Engineers Australia ([ADR 111](../adr/111-career.md)).
- **Engineering tools**: calculators, the standards library and the tools no other app owns.
- **Calendars**: Gmail and iCloud through SirisAI, without the Helmarr (Sonarr/Radarr) calendars.
- **Lightning-bolt logo**, with the classic S kept (`scripts/use-logo.sh`).
- **Mobile dock** sized for the iPhone 16 Pro Max: five items, with Career.
- **Loading cards**: Home holds every widget's place with "Loading…" until its data arrives.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull
make up
```

No database migration or manual configuration change is required.

## Versioning

From here each change adds 0.01 (fixes and small changes) or 0.1 (a new
feature or a notable change). See [docs/releases.md](../releases.md).
