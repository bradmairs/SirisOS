# SirisOS 9.62

- **Bars stay at the bottom.** On iPhone, the bars fixed to the bottom of the screen could float mid-screen until you scrolled: the tab bar, the rest timer and "Rest's over" during a workout, and the chat box. It happened after typing a weight (once the keyboard closed), and after coming back from another app, the lock screen or the rest-timer Shortcut.
  - **The old fix did nothing:** SirisOS already tried to put them back, but it "scrolled" to where the page already was, which iOS ignores.
  - **Now:** it makes a real one-pixel scroll and back, which makes iOS put them back in place. It runs once the keyboard has finished closing, and again when you come back to the app or turn the phone. It never runs while you're typing or when you've zoomed in.
