# SirisOS 6.94

- **Workouts on set days of the week.** In a routine, give each day its weekdays (Mon–Sun chips under the day's name). SirisGym → Today then shows the week (which workout is on which day) and starts today's workout. On a day with nothing on, it says "Rest day: next is Push on Monday", and you can still train. A weekday belongs to one routine day, so picking one already taken moves it. Routines without weekdays still go in turn, as before.
- The programme builder spreads its days across the week (for 3 days: Monday, Wednesday, Friday), and you can change them in the editor.
- The morning brief, gym time in the calendar, and SirisAI's "what should I do today?" follow the weekdays, rest days included.
- **SirisGYM is now SirisGym**, in the menu, page titles and everywhere it's mentioned.

Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder (SirisAI 4.18 has the rename and the rest-day answer).
