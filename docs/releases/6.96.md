# SirisOS 6.96

- **Your Watch's heart rate, set by set.** SirisGym now records the time you tick each set. When the workout is saved, it goes to SirisAI with those times. SirisAI 4.28 finds the Apple Watch workout that overlaps it (the one a start Shortcut begins with the workout) and lines its heart rate up with each set. A workout's sheet then shows **Effort, from your Watch**:
  - heart rate through the workout, with each set marked by exercise;
  - per exercise: an effort score out of 10 (from peaks against your maximum heart rate, blended with your RPE), the peak, how far heart rate came back down before the next set, and the session's best estimated 1RM;
  - the hardest and easiest exercise and the slowest recovery, in words.

  For this, Health Auto Export's **Workouts** automation needs heart-rate data turned on, so each Watch workout arrives with its heart rate over time. That's usually a point a minute, so figures for a single set are rough. Comparing exercises and sessions works well. Workouts imported, or logged before this update, have no set times.
- **Imported JEFIT workouts on the right day.** JEFIT's timestamps are UTC, and the importer treated them as local time, so a morning workout in Melbourne was filed under the day before. New imports use local time. Workouts already imported move to their local day once, when most of their times look like UTC (6pm to 4am). A workout landing on a day that already has one joins it. Nothing is lost, and a copy of gym.json is kept first (`gym.before-local-times.<time>.json`).

Needs SirisAI 4.28. Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder.
