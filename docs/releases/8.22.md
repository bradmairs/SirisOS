# SirisOS 8.22

- **Things you own shows the latest 5.** **Show all, by category** lists everything under headings: Computers and electronics, Appliances, Clothing and accessories, Furniture and home, Sport and outdoors, Car and fuel, and so on, newest first in each. Searching still shows every match.
- **Receipts work the same way** in Life Admin: the latest 5, then all of them by category.
- Categories come from each item's own name first, then its brand or shop, so a dishwasher from Harvey Norman is an appliance. Anything unclear goes under Other.
- **Expenses, Savings and Spending fold away** in Budget: the arrow on each section shows just its total. Each device remembers what you folded.
