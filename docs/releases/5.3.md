# SirisOS 5.3

**Network alerts.**

SirisOS now keeps an eye on the home network through SirisAI and the UniFi Cloud Gateway Ultra, and tells your phone when something needs you.

- **Something new joins.** You get one notification ("New on your network: espressif") with **It's mine** and **Block it** buttons. Block asks SirisAI to block it in UniFi. Until you decide, it's listed under "New on the network" on the Smart home page.
- **Something important drops off.** If a watched device is gone for 10 minutes, you get one notification per outage.
  - Watched by default: the gateway, the switches and access points, and anything named like the NAS, Home Assistant, a camera, a doorbell or a server.
  - Star any device on the Smart home page to watch it, or un-star one to stop.
- **Blocked devices** are listed with an Unblock button, which works even when the device isn't connected.

SirisOS checks every two minutes while phone notifications are on. Its first look learns what's already on the network, so you don't get an alert for every device you own. Nothing is checked in guest mode. Alerts use the "home" notification topic.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

This needs **SirisAI 3.11** or later, with UniFi set up, for the block button.
