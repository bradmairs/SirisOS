# SirisOS 7.4

The Work page, made visual.

## Work

- **The working day as a ring.** A ring fills from 8:00 to 4:30, with the time to home time in the middle, beside three tiles: work done today, time focused, and what's still due.
- **Task tiles.** Overdue (in amber when there's any), Today and This week as tiles with their counts; tap one to see its tasks. Adding a work task stays below.
- **This week.** Bars of work done each weekday (work tasks, and to-dos in work notes ticked here), today highlighted, with the week's total and focus time. Hover or tap a bar for its numbers.
- **Focus timer.** A 25-minute focus / 5-minute break timer as a ring. It keeps running if you leave the page, and each finished block adds 25 minutes to today's focus time (on the ring and in the week).
- **Apps and tools.** Tiles for your work apps with a dot for whether each answers: links in a Links group called Work, or named like GVW, CMP, Timesheet, SirisPM or Archive. The engineering calculators and Career sit beside them.
- **CPD rings.** Your hours in Engineers Australia's three-year window against 150, and a small ring for each minimum (area of practice 50, risk 10, business 15), saying what's short. **Log CPD** adds an activity with its hours and category. It's kept with your imported records, and a re-import never removes it. Add it in Engineers Australia too.
- **Work notes as cards.** The work notes changed most recently, as cards with their folder, first lines and when, above search and the jot box. Needs SirisAI 4.8 and Siris engine 2.18.
- **Laid out for a desktop.** On a wide screen the day and focus sit side by side, then tasks and the week, then apps, CPD and note to-dos, then work notes across the bottom. A phone stays one column.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 4.8 and Siris engine 2.18 (for recent work notes).

```bash
git pull && make up
```

New: `SIRISOS_WORK_PATH` (focus time and to-dos ticked, `work.json` in the data folder).
