# SirisOS 9.1

**Mental health**, a new Health tab:

- **Mood.** It comes from Apple Health's State of Mind (read by SirisAI 6.18 from Health Auto Export) or SirisOS's own quick log: Awful, Low, OK, Good or Great. You see today's mood, this week's against last, and a 30-day chart above and below neutral. For Apple Health's moods to arrive, add **State of Mind** to a Health Auto Export automation that sends to SirisAI.
- **Mindful minutes.** These come from Apple Health, plus time spent on the tools here, shown as today, this week and last week, with a 30-day chart.
- **What helps.** This compares your own days: mood after 7 hours' sleep or more against less, and on days with 30 minutes' exercise, mindful minutes or half an hour of daylight against days without. A pattern only appears when there are at least four days on each side and the difference is worth mentioning.
- **Tools:**
  - **Breathe:** box breathing, 4-7-8 or a long sigh, with a circle that paces you, for 1 to 10 minutes.
  - **Ground:** 5-4-3-2-1, one sense at a time.
  - **Reframe:** what happened, what you told yourself, the evidence, and a fairer way to see it.
  - **Three good things.**
- **Journal:** notes, reframes and good things, newest first.
- **If it's too much:** Lifeline, Beyond Blue and 000, to tap and call.

Everything you write here is kept in `mind.json` (`SIRISOS_MIND_PATH`) on SirisOS and isn't sent to SirisAI. The tab works even when SirisAI or Apple Health data isn't available.

Needs SirisAI 6.18 for Apple Health's moods.
