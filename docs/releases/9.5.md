# SirisOS 9.5

**Pay day**, in Budget and on Home:

- **A pay day for each income line.** Each salary (or any income) in Budget now has a **Pay day** box. Enter any date it's paid, past or coming, and SirisOS works out the next one from how often it comes. For example, fortnightly from Friday 30 October gives Friday 13 November, then Friday 27 November. Monthly pay on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months. The line shows "Next" with the date.
- **SirisAI reminds you.** For each income line with a pay day, SirisAI gets a reminder for 8am on the next pay day, saying what's due in and how much. It's pushed to your phone like any SirisAI reminder.
  - Change the date or the line and the reminder is moved.
  - Remove the line or the pay day and the reminder is cancelled.
  - Once a pay day has passed, the next one is set up by itself.
- **Home on the day.** A **Pay day** card shows what's due in today, and the total, on each pay day. It can be moved or hidden like any other card.

Needs SirisAI 6.71.
