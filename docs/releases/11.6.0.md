# SirisOS 11.6.0

- **"Is this right?" on Home** (with Siris 3.02 and SirisAI 4.27.0). Siris wrote 240 of its 265 notes, and you had checked none of them. The weekly message asking about three went unanswered, just as the debrief did before it became a card. This card shows one of Siris's notes at a time, the most linked first, with what Siris thinks is true: its details, a short summary and its newest update.
  - **Right** marks it checked. Siris ranks checked notes a little higher and tells SirisAI they're checked.
  - **Fix it** opens the note to edit, right on the card. Saving the fix also counts as checking it.
  - **Not now** moves on to the next note.
  - **Hidden** when there's nothing to check or SirisAI can't be reached.
- Backend: `GET /api/v1/brain/review` and `POST /api/v1/brain/reviewed` pass through to SirisAI.
