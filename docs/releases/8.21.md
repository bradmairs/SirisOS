# SirisOS 8.21

- **New tasks are for today.** A task you add goes under Today unless it says when ("pay rego Friday") or you pick a date. The Add bar now has a date, set to today; change it, or clear it for no date. This applies to project tasks and ones you say to SirisOS too.
- **Change a task's date in the list.** Each of your own tasks has a date you can change, or clear for no date.
- **Your own focus times.** The cog on Work's focus timer sets the focus and break lengths (25 and 5 until you change them). They're saved for every device, and a finished block counts its real length.
- **Choose the Work apps.** **Edit** on Apps and tools picks which of your Links show, in your order, and whether Calculators and Career appear. **Back to automatic** goes back to the Links group called Work.
