# SirisOS 9.22.0

_Was 9.8 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

- **Readiness, strain and battery rings on Home.** Three small rings: readiness out of 100, today's strain against its target, and your body battery now. Tap one for Health. They show in the morning, evening and at weekends; Customise Home and Times of day can change that.
- **Timesheet notes in Work.** Note what you worked on and for how long as you go, then fill in the real timesheet from it.
  - **Type it how you'd say it:** "Pump station review 1.5h" (or "90 min", "1:30") is enough.
  - **Codes:** add a job or project code if you like, picked from ones you've used.
  - **Totals:** each day and the week have totals, and so does each code.
  - **Copy the week:** copies the week as plain lines to paste.
  - **Other days and weeks:** pick a day to add to it, or go back a week.
- **Habits on chosen days.** A habit can be for some days only, e.g. the gym on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Pick the days when adding one, or tap "Every day" under a habit to change them.
  - **Streaks** skip the other days: they neither count nor break them.
  - **The 30-day rate** counts only the habit's days.
  - **The calendar** fades the other days.
  - **Home** only shows today's habits.
- **Tap the tab you're on to go back to the top.** Tapping the tab bar's button for the page you're already on scrolls it to the top, as in iPhone apps.
- **"Show N more" says how many are hidden.** Lists that start short, such as SirisAI's memories, said "Show all 4" (the total), which read as 4 hidden when only 1 was. They now say "Show 1 more".
- **More health insights** (with SirisAI 4.16.0). The card is now **Health insights**. A green dot means good news.
  - **The next morning:** what each thing does to your HRV and resting heart rate, e.g. "After a drink your HRV is 18% lower the next morning".
  - **Active days:** what a day of 10,000 steps or 30 minutes' exercise does to your sleep.
  - **The month:** resting heart rate, HRV, sleep and steps against the month before.
- **The morning briefing's steps** (SirisAI 4.16.0) no longer say you're thousands of steps under your usual at 7am. While today is still going, they compare yesterday's whole day instead.
