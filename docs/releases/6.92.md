# SirisOS 6.92

- **Start your Watch workout with SirisGYM.** In SirisGYM → Settings, name an iPhone Shortcut to run when a workout starts, and optionally one for when it finishes. When you tap Start, SirisGYM runs that Shortcut and comes straight back to the workout. Make the Shortcut in the Shortcuts app with exactly that name. For example, have it start a Traditional Strength Training workout on your Watch, so heart rate and kJ are recorded and matched to the session. Which workout actions Shortcuts offers depends on your iOS and watchOS versions. The finish Shortcut runs after the workout is saved, then returns to SirisGYM. It only runs on iPhone and iPad, and leaving the name empty turns it off.
