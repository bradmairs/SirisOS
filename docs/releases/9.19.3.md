# SirisOS 9.19.3

_Was 9.53 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a fix)._

- **SirisAI's briefing reads itself out.** SirisAI's daily briefing (different from SirisOS's Brief page) now arrives on Home as one tidy card, "Your briefing for Monday is ready", instead of a wall of text. Tap **Read it to me** and SirisAI reads the whole briefing in its voice; tap **Stop** to end it, and **Got it** when you're done. If SirisAI has no voice set up or can't be reached, the phone reads it with its own voice. Needs SirisAI 4.15.5.
