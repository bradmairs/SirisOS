# SirisOS 4.5

**Smart home: 13 Peck Walk in 3D.**

- **The house, floor by floor.** A new **Smart home** section (in the sidebar, and from the Home controls card) shows a 3D model of the house built from the walkthrough videos:
  - **Ground:** the front garden, the entry and stairs, the office, the store and the garage.
  - **First:** the front bedroom, the bathroom, the hallway and the main bedroom.
  - **Second:** the kitchen, dining, the lounge and the balcony.
  - **Roof:** the terrace and the covered BBQ area.

  Drag to turn it, use two fingers (or right-drag) to move, and pinch or scroll to zoom. Pick a floor to look inside it: the floors above it lift away and the ones below fade. Tap a room to see what's in it, or double-tap it to fly in.
- **Devices, and their traffic.** Everything on the network from the UniFi Cloud Gateway Ultra appears in its room, along with the Home Assistant controls. Each device is linked back through its switch or access point to the gateway, and particles run along the links the way Siris's neurons do. The busier a link, the faster and brighter it runs: cyan for down, amber for up. Rates refresh every 5 seconds. A **Network now** card shows the internet down and up rates and how many devices are on.
- **Put things where they are.** Each device goes into a room by its name ("Lounge Apple TV" goes to the lounge). Anything it can't place goes to the office, with the gateway. Choose another room in the device list or on the device's card, or hide it. Your choices are saved.

The model is an approximation from the videos: the rooms and their sizes are in `apps/web/src/house/layout.ts`.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

The live traffic needs **SirisAI 2.76** or later with UniFi set up (`SIRISAI_UNIFI_BASE_URL`, `SIRISAI_UNIFI_API_KEY`). Without it, the house still shows, with the Home Assistant controls in it.
