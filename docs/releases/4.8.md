# SirisOS 4.8

**Home: your times of day, and home-aware.**

- **Work is 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, on weekdays only.** By default:

  | Part of the day | Weekdays | Weekends |
  |---|---|---|
  | Morning | from 5:00 | from 5:00 |
  | Work (weekdays) or daytime (weekends) | from 8:30 | from 10:00 |
  | Evening | from 4:30 pm | from 5:00 pm |
  | Night | from 11:00 pm | from 11:00 pm |

- **Times of day.** A new sheet, from the link under Customise Home, sets:
  - when each part of the day starts, on weekdays and weekends;
  - which sections (Now, Today, Home) come first in each.

  It's saved on the server, so your phone and desktop follow the same day. Within a section, widgets keep the order you set in Customise Home.
- **Home controls moved to Smart home.** The lights, garage and scenes card is off the main Home page, since Siri and the Home app handle that day to day. It's kept, unchanged, on the Smart home page, under the 3D house.
- **Protocols only when you're home.** While SirisAI's presence check says you're out, Protocols are hidden and the Home section moves to the bottom. If presence isn't set up, they show as before. Cameras, car and power stay.
- The evening now leads with Today (the wrap-up) instead of Home.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

For "at home" to work, SirisAI needs your phone's UniFi name: `SIRISAI_PRESENCE_DEVICE_NAMES=iPhone - Brad` in SirisAI's .env.
