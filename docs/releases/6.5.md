# SirisOS 6.5

**Gym: a workout tracker built into SirisOS.** Under **Gym** in the menu.

- **Exercise library**: 86 exercises written for SirisOS. Each has the main muscle it works and the ones it helps, the equipment, and how it's logged: weight × reps, bodyweight reps (with optional added weight), time, or cardio (minutes and km). You can search them, filter by muscle, and add your own.
- **Routines**: build your own with days, exercises, sets, a rep range and rest, or copy a starter: full body over 3 days; push, pull, legs; upper, lower; or strength 5×5. Mark one as current, and Today offers its next day (the one after the last you did), or any other day.
- **Live workout**:
  - each exercise's sets with **last time** beside them;
  - **what to lift** (when every set reached the top of the range last time, it adds 2.5 kg; otherwise the same weight again);
  - warm-up and drop sets;
  - one tap to tick a set, which fills in last time's numbers if you left them blank;
  - **a rest timer** that starts when you tick (+15 s, skip, and a vibration at the end);
  - **plates for each side** of the bar and the estimated 1RM as you type;
  - add or remove exercises and sets, and notes.
- **Works without signal**: the workout in progress lives on the phone, so it survives the app closing. Finishing with no signal keeps it on the phone and sends it when there's a connection, and sending it twice never doubles it.
- **Records**: each exercise keeps your heaviest, best estimated 1RM, best for 1, 3, 5 and 10 reps, most reps, and biggest session. Finishing a workout lists the records it set. Each exercise has a chart of estimated 1RM and its full history.
- **History**: a month calendar of the days you trained, and each workout's sets, time, volume and records.
- **This week**: workouts, volume, weeks in a row, and working sets per muscle (a helper muscle counts half).
- Settings for kg or lb, bar weight, the plates you have, the weight step and the default rest are in the data, and will get a screen next.

## Deployment

On the server, in SirisAI's `deploy` folder:

```bash
make update
```

Or tap Update now on System status.

Also fixed: two Home tests that failed when run between about 11:30pm and midnight ("a meeting in 25 minutes" fell on tomorrow). They now run at a fixed time of day.
