# SirisOS 12.2.3

**SirisTrails, polished after a look on a phone.**

- **Walks show their own local time.** Apple Health's export writes every time in the phone's time zone when it was exported, so a Hong Kong hike showed Melbourne times. Each walk's time zone now comes from where it was: the state for Australia and parts of the US (Byron Bay keeps Sydney time), and the nearest time-zone city elsewhere.
- **The elevation chart is readable on a phone:** taller, with heights and distances in normal-sized text, the range written above it ("24 m to 490 m"), and a dot that follows your finger.
- **Splits:** the Time column, which repeated the pace, is gone. A short last split shows its distance and time, and only whole kilometres can be "fastest".
- **The kilometre markers on the map are centred.**
- **Tapping a country or state scrolls the map into view,** so you see it zoom there.
