# SirisOS 9.21.0

_Was 9.7 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

- **A picture for each exercise in SirisGym.** Open an exercise to see real photos of the start and end of the movement, playing in turn so you can see how it's done. If you've turned on "reduce motion" on your iPhone, they're shown side by side instead. During a workout, a small picture sits beside each exercise's name; tap it for the big one.
  - **Where they come from:** Free Exercise DB, a public-domain set of 873 exercises with two photos each. The built-in exercises are matched by hand.
  - **Your own and imported exercises:** matched by name, and the equipment never disagrees (a barbell exercise never gets a dumbbell picture). An exercise with no close match gets no picture rather than a wrong one.
  - **Change it on the exercise's page:**
    - **Choose another picture** searches the library.
    - **Use my photo** takes your own, e.g. the machine at your gym. It's kept on your server only.
    - **No picture** turns it off.
    - **Automatic** goes back to the matched one.
  - **Offline:** each picture is fetched the first time it's shown and kept on your server, so after that it works without the internet. Your server needs to reach github.com for that first fetch.
