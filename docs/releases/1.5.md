# SirisOS 1.5

- **Phone notifications:** SirisOS can now send real notifications to your iPhone (or Mac). Turn them on in the Inbox under **Notifications on this device** and pick what for: an app goes down, alerts and approvals from SirisAI, your daily brief is ready (7 am by default), and career reminders (a goal due within a week, CPD hours about to drop out of your 3-year window). Tap a notification to open the right screen. Each notification is sent once; "app is down" comes back if it goes down again later.
- Notifications are sent directly by SirisOS using standard Web Push. Nothing goes through a third-party service other than Apple's or the browser's own push service.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull
make up
```

Optionally set `SIRISOS_VAPID_SUBJECT=mailto:<your email>` in `.env`.

On iPhone, notifications need SirisOS added to the Home Screen from an HTTPS address whose certificate the phone trusts. See "Phone notifications" in `docs/deploy-hub.md`.
