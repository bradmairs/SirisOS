# SirisOS 4.9

**Home through the day.** Each part of the day now shows what matters then, not just the same widgets in a different order. "Show everything" brings the rest back for the visit.

| | Starts | Leads with | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | 5:00 | your first event (and when to leave for it), today's weather (with a warning if it'll rain), your top 3 | Tasks, Coming up this morning, parcels, protocols |
| **Work** (weekdays) | 8:30 | the next meeting with a countdown, your top 3 to tick off | work tasks, Coming up, Career, Engineering and the other app widgets, Learned today |
| **Weekend daytime** | 10:00 | what's still on today, and the weather outside | your tasks, Fitness, parcels, the car, protocols, cameras, power, the home server |
| **Evening** | 4:30 pm weekdays, 5 pm weekends | the wrap-up, then tomorrow's top 3, and a welcome home if you've just got in | your tasks, tonight's and tomorrow morning's events, Fitness, the car if it needs charging, power, protocols |
| **Wind down** | 8 pm | tomorrow: the first event, when to leave, the weather, your top 3 | what the brain learned today, and a box to empty your head into. The page is dimmer and quieter. |
| **Night** | 11 pm | the time and tomorrow's first event | nothing else |

- **Only when they need you:**
  - **Home server:** shows when it's busy, nearly full or hot. It always shows on weekends.
  - **Siris apps:** shows when one is down.
  - **Power:** shows when power is cheap or jobs are waiting.
  - **The car:** shows in the evening only if it needs charging.
- **Work and personal tasks.** Each task is work or personal. Home shows work tasks in work hours and your own the rest of the time. Unlabelled tasks are guessed from their words (a GVW report, an RFI or a pump station is work). Tap the Work or Personal chip on a task in Tasks to change it.
- **Your top 3 tick off on Home:** in the morning and at work, a top-3 item that's on your task list can be ticked there.
- **Welcome home.** When you get home in the evening, the card says what's waiting: parcels, and your inbox. This needs SirisAI 2.89, which says when you arrived.
- **Times of day** now also sets:
  - when wind down starts;
  - how early to leave for an event with a place (30 minutes by default; traffic isn't taken into account);
  - which widgets each part of the day shows.

The schedule you saved in 4.8 keeps its times, and gains wind down at 8 pm.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

The welcome home needs SirisAI 2.89.
