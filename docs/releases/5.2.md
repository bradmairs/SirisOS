# SirisOS 5.2

**Home maintenance, in the house.**

The Smart home page has two new cards, **Maintenance** and **Appliances**, and the rooms in the 3D house now show what needs doing.

- **Jobs that repeat.** Each job has a room and a "how often", for example "Clean the AC filters, every 3 months". It comes due that long after you last did it.
  - **Done** makes today the last time.
  - **Next week** puts it off seven days.
  - Tap a job to change it, link it to an appliance, add notes, or delete it.
- **A starting list for 13 Peck Walk:**
  - the AC filters
  - testing the smoke alarms and replacing their batteries
  - the gutters and roof drains
  - the BBQ's gas bottle, hoses and grill
  - the dishwasher and rangehood filters
  - the washing machine's clean cycle
  - the balcony drain
  - the car service and the garage door

  None of these is due until you say when you last did it. Enter a date against each one under "Not started".
- **Appliances.** For each one: its room, make and model, when you bought it, its warranty, and its manual (a link, or a PDF or photo you upload). A warranty with 30 days or less left is flagged.
- **In the house.** Rooms with an overdue job glow orange, and rooms with one due today glow amber. Tap a room to see its jobs and appliances, and tick jobs off there.
- **On Home.** A new **Around the house** card shows the jobs due today or overdue, with Done buttons. It's hidden when nothing is due. It shows in the evening and at weekends; change that in Home → Times of day.
- **Notifications.** You get one on the morning a job comes due, with **Done** and **Next week** buttons, and one 30 days before a warranty ends. They use a new "home" topic. Phones already set to get everything get it automatically, and you can turn it off in Notifications.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Uploaded manuals are kept in `manuals/` in the data folder, next to `maintenance.json`, so backups include them.
