# SirisOS 11.2.1

_Was 10.61 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a fix)._

- **Every screen opens at its top.** Moving between screens kept the last screen's scroll position, so Health (for example) could open part-way down, with its title and the Health and Fitness tabs out of sight. Screens now open at the top whether you get there from the menu, the tab bar, a section's tabs or a link.
  - **Back and forward** still return you to where you were.
  - **Tabs within a screen** (Health's Today, Sleep and Vitals, for example) keep your place.
- **Wind down's error, explained.** "That didn't work: [Errno 113] No route to host" was SirisAI failing to reach Home Assistant when it tried to dim the lights. SirisAI 4.24.1 now sends the wind-down reminder anyway, and says plainly that the lights weren't dimmed because Home Assistant couldn't be reached.

To update: `make update` (and update SirisAI to 4.24.1).
