# SirisOS 4.93

**Siris (the brain): read whole notes from search, and the mind map below the insights.**

- **Notes open in place.** In "Search your notes", tap a result to read the whole note right there, and tap again to fold it away. It shows:
  - the note's formatting: headings, lists, tick boxes, tables, quotes and links;
  - when it was last updated, and its tags;
  - the notes it's connected to.
- **Links lead on.** A `[[link]]` in a note, or one of its connected notes, searches for that note and opens it.
- **The mind map** is now below Siris's insights, after Quick capture and Learned today, instead of at the top.
