# SirisOS 4.31

- **SirisAI's approval PIN works in SirisOS.** When an action needs your PIN, such as **Install updates**, **Reboot** or **Restart**, the Inbox now asks for it, sends it with the action, and says if it's wrong. Before this you got "pin_required" and the action couldn't be approved from SirisOS at all.
- The same goes for the page a notification opens.
- SirisAI's other refusals (wrong PIN, locked out, an expired approval) now reach the app as they are, instead of as a generic error.
