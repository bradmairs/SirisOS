# SirisOS 12.2.1

- **New walks and hikes are named straight away.** When a walk arrives from Health Auto Export, or from an uploaded Apple Health export, naming starts at once instead of at the next 10-minute run. It gets its place, state and country, and its name: the peak, track, park or town. The 10-minute run still catches anything missed, and only one naming run goes at a time. A walk you've renamed keeps your name.
