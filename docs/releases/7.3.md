# SirisOS 7.3

What SirisAI 4.6 added, in SirisOS. **Update SirisAI to 4.6 first**: these screens show its data.

## Life admin (new screen, in the menu)

- **Letters and bills**: **Add** takes a photo or PDF of a letter, bill or form, or a text file. SirisAI reads it on your own server, files it in Siris with the file embedded, and sets reminders 3 days before anything that's due and on the day. The card says what it found ("Due Fri 20 Nov ($412.50); 2 reminders set"). Search finds letters by sender or words; tap one for its summary and to remove it.
- **Bills and subscriptions**: renewals, free trials ending, price rises and bills due that SirisAI found in your email. **Check email** looks now (it also checks every morning). **Done** or **Ignore** closes one.
- **Trips**: trips in the calendar for the next three weeks, with the weather where you're going, a packing list and a house list. **Away while I'm gone** makes away mode run by itself during the trip. Doors stay locked when you're back.

## Approve in SirisOS

When SirisAI pushes an approval that needs the PIN, the notification's **Approve in SirisOS** button opens the Inbox on that approval with the PIN box ready. Nothing is sent until you enter the PIN. If it was answered already, the Inbox says so.

## Conversations

SirisAI's **Conversations** sheet has a search: words, and a date to search from. Results show the title and short summary SirisAI wrote for each conversation.

## SirisAI speed (System status)

A new card shows how long SirisAI usually takes, and its slowest 5%, for each part of a reply: the first word, the whole reply, the model, gathering context, choosing tools, running the tool and writing the answer. It also says how many recent replies were home commands answered without the model, and names any background job that failed or is running slow.

## Behind it

- `GET/POST /api/v1/life/documents`, `GET/DELETE /api/v1/life/documents/{id}`, `GET /api/v1/life/bills`, `POST /api/v1/life/bills/scan`, `POST /api/v1/life/bills/{id}/status`, `GET /api/v1/life/travel`, `POST /api/v1/life/travel/away` and `GET /api/v1/life/speed` pass SirisAI's data through. `GET /api/v1/assistant/conversations/search` does the same for conversation search.
- SirisAI is told where SirisOS is through `SIRISAI_SIRISOS_PUBLIC_URL`. The `deploy/` stack sets it to `https://<SIRIS_HOST>:8444`.
