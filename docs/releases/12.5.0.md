# SirisOS 12.5.0

**SirisTrails: photos on the route, GPX, a picture to share, and tidying up recordings.**

## Photos

**Add photos** on a walk's page and pick them from your phone. Each one goes on the route where you were when you took it:

- **Placing:** at the photo's own GPS when it has one, else at the point on the route at the time it was taken. Photos taken well before or after the walk are kept in the walk's gallery but not put on the map.
- **On the walk's page:** the gallery runs in order along the route, with each photo's distance. A camera marks each one on the map, and tapping a photo opens it full screen, where it can be removed.
- **Sizes:** your phone makes a 2048 px copy and a small thumbnail before uploading, so originals aren't sent and SirisOS needs no image tools.

Immich isn't running at the moment (its last database backup is from June 2026, and the NAS holds only its thumbnails), so photos don't come in from Immich automatically yet.

## Share

- **Share a picture:** an image of the walk, with its shape, name, distance, time, climb, pace and elevation, opened in the iPhone share sheet. It uses no map, and the route leaves off the start and end near home, so it doesn't show where you live.
- **GPX:** the walk as a GPX file for other apps. **GPX without home** leaves off the parts within 400 m of home at each end.

## Tidy up

- **Trim:** cut the start or end, for example when the phone kept recording in the car. Distance, time, climb and the map line are worked out again. The whole recording is kept, and **Undo trim** puts the walk back exactly as it was.
- **Join:** two recordings of one walk on the same day (paused and restarted) become one. Distance and climb come from the joined route, and the time is both recordings added together. **Split again** brings the second walk back as it was.
