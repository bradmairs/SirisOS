# SirisOS 11.8.0

**Smoother scrolling, a fuel reminder that comes, habits in Wrap Up, and receipts to download.**

## Smoother scrolling, everywhere

Scrolling a workout stuttered, showed half-drawn cards and jumped back. There were three causes.

- **The glass:** every card blurred the wallpaper behind it, live, and the wallpaper stays put while the page moves, so an iPhone had to blur every card again on every frame of a scroll. The wallpaper is soft and nearly plain, so the cards in the page now paint the same tint with no live blur, and look the same. The bars that stay put over moving content (the dock, the top bar, the menu, sheets, the chat box) keep the real glass.
- **The jump back:** the 9.62 fix that keeps the dock in place on iPhone nudged the page by a pixel when iOS said the visible area moved. That also happens mid-scroll and in the bounce at the top, and a nudge then stopped the scroll and threw the page back. It now waits until you've stopped touching and the page has been still for a moment.
- **The workout clock:** the whole workout screen redrew every second to tick the clock. Now only the clock and the rest timer tick.

## The fuel reminder

There was no fuel notification on Wednesday morning. The 10.6 notification filter drops a notification whose card you've already seen in the app. The fuel card is on Home from 6pm the evening before, under the same name as the 5:30am notification, so seeing it on Tuesday evening cancelled Wednesday's reminder. Two more rules could also stop it reaching your phone: with SirisOS open on another screen it became a banner there instead, and it went only to the device used last.

- Reminders at a time you chose (fuel, tasks, bedtime nudges, wind-down, travel, the timesheet and others) now:
  - only count as seen if you saw them in the last two hours;
  - are pushed even while SirisOS is open somewhere;
  - go to every device.
- If prices can't be checked on the morning (Fair Fuel down, no key), the card and the 5:30am notification say so, instead of not being there.

Two notification tests were set on 24 November and had started failing once that day passed. They now run at their own time.

## Habits in Wrap Up

Wrap Up has **Today's habits**, after Today: tick the ones you did, with a warning on any streak that would end tonight. It says "All done today" when they're ticked. It only shows the habits for today's days.

## Download receipts

- **One receipt:** **Download** on any receipt in Life Admin saves its own file (the invoice PDF, the photo, or the email when the receipt is in the email), named by date and shop: "2026-11-03 Bunnings - Cordless drill.pdf".
- **Many:** above the receipts list, choose the ones shown (all, or a search), this financial year or last financial year, and **Download** a zip of them with `receipts.csv` (date, from, what, summary, file).
- **On iPhone** it opens the share sheet (Save to Files, AirDrop, Mail). If the zip took a while, tap **Save** to open it.
- An email saved this way opens as a plain page: nothing in it can run or load.

New: `GET /api/v1/life/documents/{id}/download`, `GET /api/v1/life/receipts/download?q=&ids=&start=&end=`.
