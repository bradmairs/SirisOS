# SirisOS 9.3

- **Habits on Home.** A Home card ticks today's habits in one tap. From 6pm, a streak not yet kept today says "Keep your 6-day streak". The card only appears once you have a habit, and it can be moved or hidden like any other.
- **Habits in "What helps".** Your Apple Health mood is compared on days you ticked each habit against days you didn't, from the day the habit started.
- **Mind and habits in the weekly review.** A new card shows this week's mood against last week and mindful minutes (both from Apple Health), plus each habit's days done out of days possible.
- **Documents and renewals**, in Life. Add a passport, driver's licence, car rego, insurance, Medicare card, warranty or membership with its expiry date. They're listed soonest first. Reminders come 60, 30 and 7 days before and on the day, from 9am. Anything due within a week shows on Home's Today timeline. When you renew, change the date in place. Kept in `renewals.json` (`SIRISOS_RENEWALS_PATH`).
- **An iPhone Home Screen widget.** Set it up on the Security page:
  1. Make a widget key there.
  2. Copy the Scriptable script it gives you.
  3. In Scriptable, make a new script, paste it in, and add a Scriptable widget to your Home Screen.

  The widget shows the next event, tasks due, readiness, your next workout, habits, a renewal due within 30 days, and a rest timer that counts down live. The key can only read this summary. Making a new key stops the old one, and **Stop the widget** turns it off.
- **Faster to open.** Home loaded all of Health, Training, Work, Maintenance, SirisGym's cards and the inbox just for their Home cards. They now load when shown, and the app's main download falls from 369 KB to 212 KB (100 KB to 62 KB compressed).
- **Setup checklist.** System status now checks that mood (State of Mind) and mindful minutes are arriving from Apple Health, and that a passkey is set up so the journal locks.
