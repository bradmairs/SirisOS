# SirisOS 11.9.0

**SirisGym: faint numbers from last time, removing sets and warm-ups, a way back from Shortcuts, and effort that knows each lift.**

- **Last session's numbers, faintly.** A new workout's weight and reps boxes start empty, showing last session's numbers for that set faintly (or the suggestion, the first time you do a lift). The sets still to do stand out from the ones done, which show in full.
  - Tap a box and type: there's nothing to delete first. A box that already has a number selects it, so typing replaces it.
  - Tick a set without typing and it logs the faint numbers.
  - A weight you type still carries down to the sets below.
  - The plates for the bar show for the faint weight too.
  - What SirisGym suggests for today (e.g. "add 2.5") stays in the line above the sets.
- **Removing sets and warm-ups.** An exercise's ⋯ menu has two new options:
  - **Remove sets:** each set's tick becomes a bin. Tap a bin to remove that set (a ticked set asks first), then **Done**.
  - **Remove warm-ups:** removes the warm-ups not yet done.

  Swiping a set left still works too.
- **Coming back from the Watch Shortcut.** Apple gives Home Screen web apps no link back. A link back opens Safari, without your workout, which is why 8.6 stopped asking for one. There are now two ways to come back automatically:
  1. **In the Shortcut:** end your start Shortcut with **Open App** and choose SirisOS, if your iPhone lists it there.
  2. **A return link:** in SirisGym's Settings, put in a link that opens SirisOS's web app on your iPhone (`{url}` stands for the page), then tap **Try it**. Try it runs nothing: Shortcuts comes straight back by the link, and you see whether you land back in the app or in Safari. The link is kept on this iPhone only.

  Without either, tap ◀ SirisOS at the top left, as before.
- **Effort against each lift's own range** (SirisAI 4.28). Not every lift raises your heart rate much, so against your maximum heart rate, lifts like curls always read "easy". Effort now comes from where a session's peaks sit between the lowest and highest seen for that exercise:
  - the lowest reads 2 and the highest 10, and the range moves as new highs and lows come in;
  - until a lift has 3 earlier sessions, it's rated against your maximum, as before;
  - the advice says a lift "raised your heart rate less than it usually does", with its range.

  Workouts already rated keep their score. New ones, and any analysed again, use the new range.

## Update

`make update` on the server (it updates SirisAI too), then open SirisOS once so the new version loads. A workout already in progress keeps its filled-in numbers; the next one starts with the faint ones.

Not yet tried on an iPhone: the faint numbers and removing sets were checked in the tests and in screenshots in Chromium; a return link has to be tried on your iPhone.
