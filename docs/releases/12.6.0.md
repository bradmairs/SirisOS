# SirisOS 12.6.0

**SirisTrails: a heatmap, the streets you've explored, a wishlist of walks, and your walks across Siris.**

## Heatmap

The map has **Routes** and **Heatmap** buttons. The heatmap draws every walk faintly on top of each other, so the places you walk most glow brightest. Zoomed out, each walk's start glows too, so a whole country still shows where you go.

## Streets explored

For the suburbs you walk in most (five walks or more), SirisTrails finds the named streets and paths inside the suburb's boundary from OpenStreetMap. It then shows how much of them you've walked: how many streets, how many kilometres, and the share. Tap a suburb to see its streets on the map, green where you've walked and red dashes where you haven't yet.

- One suburb is looked up every ten minutes in the background, and each is refreshed every two months.
- The share walked is worked out again whenever your walks change.

## Wishlist

Search for a track, peak or park, and add it to your wishlist. Results near home come first.

- **For each one:** its length and climb (from OpenStreetMap and Open-Meteo's elevation service), shown dashed in purple on the map.
- **Whole tracks:** a track is looked up whole, not just the piece of it the search found. When OpenStreetMap's servers are busy, the full track is filled in by a later background run.
- **Ticked off by itself:** a wish is ticked off when one of your walks covers 70% of the track, reaches the peak, or walks inside the park. The ticked item links to that walk.

## Across Siris

- **Home:** a **Walks** card with your last walk (with its weather and how many times you've walked that route) and the year so far. It shows when you've walked in the last 60 days, and can be moved or hidden like the other cards.
- **The monthly recap** adds your walks: how many, the distance and climb, the longest, and new places, against the month before.
- **SirisAI:** SirisOS's facts gain a `walks` topic, so SirisAI can answer "how far did I walk this year" or "when did I last walk Mount Lofty". That needs the matching SirisAI update.
