# SirisOS 6.8

SirisGYM, round two: training that knows how you're doing.

- **Sets per muscle against a target.** This week's working sets for each muscle (a helper muscle counts half) against 10-20 a week, with the range shaded. It names the muscles under the range, and any over it.
- **Deloads.** SirisGYM suggests a lighter week when your main lifts have stalled for three sessions, after six or more weeks of training in a row, or when readiness has been low on three of the last five mornings. "Start a deload week" halves the sets and takes 10% off for seven days, and those sessions don't set records.
- **Readiness in the plan.** On a Recover morning, a routine day starts a set lighter and 5% easier, and says why. On a Primed one it tells you it's a good day to push.
- **Gym sessions count.** Each finished workout goes to SirisAI, so it counts in the day's strain (not twice if your Watch also recorded it). It also ticks off the training plan's strength days.
- **Apple Watch heart rate.** A workout shows the Watch workout that overlaps it: average and maximum heart rate, kJ and minutes.
- **Progress tab.** Strength: each main lift's best estimated 1RM against your bodyweight (from Apple Health), with a rough level and this year against last. The year in review: workouts, hours and tonnes lifted against last year, volume by month with records marked, best lifts and most-trained muscles. Your year shows the gym too.
- **Fix:** a workout is dated by your local day. A morning session used to be filed under the day before, because the phone sends UTC.

Needs SirisAI 4.06. Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder.
