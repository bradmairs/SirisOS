# SirisOS 1.3

- **Phone top bar:** the SirisOS logo and name sit a little lower, on a solid background. Before, the bar faded out through its middle, so under an iPhone's Dynamic Island the name and logo looked washed out.
- **Tap the logo to go Home:** the SirisOS logo and name, in the phone top bar and at the top of the sidebar, now take you to Home.
- **Siris App Suite on phones:** your apps are now a swipeable row near the top of Home, just under "Ask Siris", instead of a grid at the very bottom. Wider screens keep the grid at the bottom.
- **"Apps" is now "Siris App Suite"** on Home, on Engineering and in the sidebar.
- **SirisPM removed from SirisOS:** the Project Management app tile, its Home widget, its sidebar link, its search results, its overdue tasks in the inbox and the daily brief, and its `APD_PM_*` settings are gone. SirisPM itself (`:8069`) is unchanged.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull
make up
```

Any `APD_PM_*` lines left in `.env` are now ignored and can be deleted.
