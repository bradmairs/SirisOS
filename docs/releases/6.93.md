# SirisOS 6.93

Health data you can trust, and more from SirisGYM. Goes with SirisAI 4.17.

- **Test the sync** (Training → Apple Health). It sends a sample export for this hour through SirisOS and SirisAI, keeps nothing, and says whether that path works. If it passes and today is still empty, the phone isn't sending. Each attempt in the list now shows the days it covered.
- **Where the numbers come from** (Health → Data). For any day: each metric, the value that counts, which source it came from, the other sources' values, and that day's sleep and workouts.
- **Safer syncs and faster alerts** (SirisAI 4.17). A sync split into several requests now adds up instead of replacing the day. You get a push when Health Auto Export hasn't sent for 6 hours (10am to 10pm), or when it's sending without today in it.
- **Swap an exercise** mid-workout. Tap ⇄ for alternatives that do the same movement, then ones for the same muscle, with last time and a weight to try.
- **Protein target** (SirisGYM → Today). 1.6 g/kg of bodyweight, or 2.0 on a training day (both adjustable), against what Apple Health and meal photos have counted today.
- **Find gym time today.** It finds the first free hour (early, lunch, after work, then any time from 8am to 9pm) and adds "Gym: <today's day>" to your calendar when you confirm.
- **The gym in the morning brief.** Today's routine day with what to lift, adjusted for readiness or a deload, and how long since your last workout.
- **Live check.** `make verify` (the verification script) now checks SirisGYM, when the phone last sent health data and whether today was in it, and runs a sample export through SirisAI.

Needs SirisAI 4.17. Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder.
