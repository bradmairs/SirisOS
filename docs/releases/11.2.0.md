# SirisOS 11.2.0

_Was 10.6 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

**Smarter notifications.** You should get fewer, and the right ones.

- **Nothing you've already seen.** A card, inbox item or screen you've looked at in SirisOS isn't sent as a notification. If it was waiting for the roundup, it's taken out. This covers bins, fuel, the weekend, the garden, the bedroom, catch-ups, slow internet, the monthly recap, inbox items, the brief, both wrap-ups and the weekly review.
- **Still true.** Just before something waiting goes out, it's checked again. If an inbox item has been dealt with, or the garden has been watered, it isn't sent.
- **A banner while you're using it.** While SirisOS is open on any device, notifications that aren't urgent show as a banner at the top of the app instead of buzzing your phone. Tap the banner to open it. It goes by itself after ten seconds.
- **One device.** A notification goes to the device you used SirisOS on last, not to all of them. Urgent ones still go to every device.
- **Quiet when you're asleep or busy.**
  - **Asleep:** between tonight's bedtime and when you woke this morning (from Health), anything not urgent waits for the morning roundup.
  - **Busy:** during a calendar event or a running focus block, it waits until that ends.
- **Home things at home.** Bins, garden and bedroom notifications wait until someone's home, so if you're out at 7pm on bin night, it reminds you when you get in.
- **It learns what you ignore.** If you don't open five of a kind in a row, that kind moves to the roundup. Notifications settings show the week (sent, opened and spared) for each kind, and you can set each one to Automatic, Always, Roundup only or Off.
- **A daily cap.** At most 6 notifications that aren't urgent per day; the rest wait for the roundup. You can change the number.
- **Replacing, not stacking.** A newer notification of the same kind replaces the older one on the lock screen.

Some things still come straight away, unless you've already seen them:
- urgent alerts
- things at a time you chose: tasks, the fuel stop, bedtime and wind-down, trips, the Friday timesheet, money before pay

Turn it all off with **Smarter notifications** in Notifications settings.

**On iPhone,** SirisOS needs to have been opened once on each device after updating. That's how it learns which device is which.

To update: `make update`.
