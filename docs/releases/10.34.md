# SirisOS 10.34

- **Mealie connects with an address like 192.168.0.100:9000.** If `SIRISOS_MEALIE_URL` has no `http://`, SirisOS now adds it. Before, Meals said "Mealie can't be reached (UnsupportedProtocol)".
- **Clearer Mealie errors:** they now say what's wrong: a malformed address, nothing answering at the address, or Mealie being too slow.

To update: `make update`.
