# SirisOS 9.65

- **Percentages of your best, on every exercise.** Open an exercise in SirisGym and tap **Show percentages of your best** under the chart. You get 95% down to 50% of your best estimated 1RM:
  - **Each weight** is rounded to what your current gym can load (its plates, dumbbells or machine stack).
  - **Reps:** each row says about how many reps that weight is good for. Below about 60%, it just says "12+ reps", where the estimate stops holding.
  - **Assisted exercises:** each row is the help that leaves you lifting that share of your best.
  - **Where it's left out:** exercises with nothing logged yet, and bodyweight, timed and cardio exercises.
