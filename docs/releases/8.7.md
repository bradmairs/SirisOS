# SirisOS 8.7

SirisGym, from a full review: tested on a copy of Brad's real data at iPhone size.

## Your history, linked
- **Link your history.** Your routine used built-in exercises (Back Squat, Lat Pulldown, Face Pull…) while six years of JEFIT history sat under your own names (Barbell Squat, Cable Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip), Cable Rope Face Pull…). So SirisGym said "No history yet: pick a weight" for lifts you've done more than 100 times. Today now lists each one with its likely match. **Use it** points the routine at your history; **Keep as is** stops asking. Workouts aren't changed.
- **Tidy imported exercises** (Exercises). 33 of your imported exercises had the wrong kind, equipment or muscle: Back Hyperextension "works triceps", Sit-Up "weight × reps", 43 with equipment "other". Fix one at a time, or **Fix all**, with a copy of your gym file saved first. **Edit** on any of your own exercises changes its name, muscle, equipment and how it's logged.
- **Duplicate suggestions go by equipment.** Machine Bench Press, Dumbbell Incline Bench Press and Barbell Preacher Curl are no longer offered for merging into the barbell or other versions.

## Typos
- **A check before a typo goes in.** Ticking a set far above your heaviest in the last year (more than 1.5×) asks first: "210 kg? That's well over your heaviest lately (90 kg)."
- **Fix a past set.** In an exercise's history, **Fix** corrects one workout's weights and reps. A set far above the rest is marked "looks like a typo", like the 210 kg squat on 21 September (estimated 1RM 266 kg).

## The workout, on the phone
- The title is the day ("Legs"), with the routine small underneath.
- Each exercise's extras sit behind **⋯**: up, down, swap, superset, note, film and remove. More of the workout fits on the screen.
- **Swipe** a set right to tick it, or left to delete it. Every set can now be deleted, not just barbell ones.
- **Plates** for each side show under every barbell set.
- Beside each set, **how it compares with last time** ("+2.5 kg on last time", "+1 rep"), and a **New best** badge when a set beats your best estimated 1RM.
- **After finishing:** the change since the last time you did that day (sets, volume and minutes), and **How did it feel?** with one tap.

## Today
- **Repeat last** does your last workout again: the same exercises and sets, with the weights and reps filled in.
- **Do this again** on any workout in History does the same.
- **Recovery** shows each muscle, when you last trained it, and how recovered it is: about a day or so for small muscles, two for the upper body, three for the legs.
- **"Not now"** on the deload card puts it away for four weeks.
- "Under target" now goes by how far through the week it is, so a Wednesday doesn't say every muscle is behind.
- **A Sunday evening notification** with the week: workouts, records, any muscles short of sets, and your next workout.

## Smaller fixes
- **Warm-ups for barbell lifts only**, at least 10 kg apart: 20, 35, 47.5 before a 60 kg squat, not four sets, and none before calf raises or dumbbell work.
- **Timed and bodyweight exercises get their own advice.** Plank says "Last time up to 60 s: aim for 60 s", and Pull-up "Last time 10, 10, 10 reps: aim for 15", instead of "pick a weight".
- **A workout with a start that isn't a date** is refused. Before, one could be saved and then stop every gym page loading. Any already in the file are set aside, not deleted.
- **A workout in the future** is refused, and an end before the start is dropped.
- **An exercise SirisGym doesn't know** is kept as one of yours, to name later.
- **Lift goals.** Year goals can track a lift ("Bench 120 kg") from SirisGym's best estimated 1RM this year.
