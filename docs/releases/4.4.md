# SirisOS 4.4

A tidier SirisOS and a tidier Siris.

- **Yearly goals, on Your year.** Set them in January; SirisOS asks once at the start of the year. Running, CPD hours, journal days and tasks done count themselves. Other goals are numbers you count up yourself ("Read 12 books") or done-or-not ("Get chartered"). Each goal shows how far along it is against how far through the year you are, and whether it's **On track** or **Behind**. Earlier years are a tap away.
- **Tasks are just yours.** The list is now the few things you want to get done: what you add, your wrap-up's top 3, and what you say to SirisOS. It no longer copies every `- [ ]` in your Siris notes or your SirisAI reminders, so it doesn't double up on Apple Reminders. A project's page still shows the tasks in its note.
- **Projects are personal.** Your life projects and the home lab only. Work projects (anything in the vault's Work folder, or with CMP or GVW in the name) are left out, and project pages no longer show emails. Set `SIRISOS_PROJECTS_EXCLUDE` to change the words.
- **The wrap-up doesn't make day notes.** Its line about your day and tomorrow's top 3 go to Siris as memories, filed onto the notes they're about.
- **Archived:**
  - **Meetings** (and the 10-minute heads-up and Prepare buttons)
  - **Email** (SirisAI's drafted replies no longer appear in the Inbox)
  - **iPhone shortcuts** (with the Scriptable widget and the shortcut keys)
  - the **This week** screen

  Your personal calendar is still on Home, in the brief and on project pages. The archived code can come back from the `v4.31` tag if you ever want it.

## Also

- **Siris 1.63:** the old daily notes, the "Day 2026-10-23" note and the three notes made from calendar events are deleted. The useful line from the day note is now on Career.
- **SirisAI 2.66:** calendar events never become notes.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Update SirisAI to 2.66 too, so the wrap-up's memories are filed properly.
