# SirisOS 9.20.0

_Was 9.6 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

SirisGym: assisted and one-side exercises counted right, what makes a good session, and a card to share a workout.

- **Assisted exercises.** On an assisted pull-up or dip machine, the weight is help, so less is harder. SirisGym now counts what you actually lifted: your bodyweight (from Apple Health) less the help.
  - **Records:** "least help ever" is a record. More help never is.
  - **Best estimated 1RM:** worked out from what you lifted.
  - **Volume:** counts what you lifted, not the help.
  - **The exercise's chart:** goes up as the help comes down.
  - **What to lift next:** takes help off when every set reaches the reps, and says when you're ready for the unassisted version.
  - **Tired days and deload weeks:** add help rather than taking weight off.
  - **Live workout:** the weight column is headed "Help kg", and a new best is worked out the same way.
- **One side at a time.** For single-arm rows, split squats, single-leg press and the like, you log one side's weight and reps, and the volume counts both sides. The best estimated 1RM stays per side.
- **Both are worked out from the exercise's name** ("Assisted Pull-Up", "Single Arm Cable Row", "Bulgarian Split Squat"). Change either on the exercise's page, under its name; built-in exercises included.
- **What makes a good session** (Progress tab, and the Sunday review). Each workout is scored against your best for the same lifts in the 90 days before. Your best and worst sessions are then compared by:
  - sleep the night before
  - resting heart rate against your usual
  - morning or later
  - caffeine that day

  This gives lines like "You lift about 4% more after 7 hours' sleep or more than after less". A finding needs at least four workouts on each side and a gap of 2% or more; until then it says there isn't enough yet. Sleep, heart rate and caffeine come from Apple Health through SirisAI.
- **Share a workout.** When you finish, and on any saved workout, there's a card showing the title, date, minutes, sets, the weight lifted, each exercise's best set, new bests and the Watch's heart rate. Tap **Share workout card** to send it or save it to Photos (on a computer it downloads). It's drawn on the phone; nothing leaves SirisOS unless you share it.
