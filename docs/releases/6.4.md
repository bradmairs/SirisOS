# SirisOS 6.4

**Strain, a week of sleep, a Health Auto Export check, one-tap updates, the standby killer and asking your manuals.** Needs SirisAI 4.03.

- **Strain** on Health → Today: how hard your body worked today, 0 to 21, against a target band from this morning's readiness (Primed 14–18, Steady 10–14, Recover 4–9). It also shows each workout's strain and the week as bars.
- **Your week of sleep** on Health → Sleep and in the weekly review: the average against what you need, how short you were, how much your bedtime varied, the best and shortest nights, what went with shorter sleep, and one thing to try next week.
- **Health Auto Export check** on Training → Apple Health. It reads what the last exports looked like and names the setting to change: "set Aggregate to Hours", "set the date range to Previous 7 Days", or "add a Workouts automation". When everything's right, it says so.
- **Update now** on System status → Upkeep. When a new SirisAI is out, one tap asks the server's updater for it: SirisAI and SirisOS rebuild and restart, and roll back on their own if they don't come back healthy. **Check** looks for a release now. If the updater isn't running on the server, it says so and what to run once (`make auto-update`).
- **Standby killer** on the Power card. For each always-on device that has a Home Assistant smart plug, tick "Off overnight and when nobody's home". It's switched off between 1am and 6am (`SIRISOS_STANDBY_HOURS`) and whenever SirisAI's presence says the house is empty, then back on afterwards. Only plugs it turned off go back on, and one you switch back on by hand isn't fought.
- **Ask the manuals** on the Appliances card: ask "how do I descale the coffee machine?" and get the answer from the PDF manuals you uploaded, with the page to look at. It reads the PDF's text, so scanned image-only manuals can't be searched.
- **A pre-push check** for development: `scripts/check.sh` runs the backend tests, types and web tests (`VISUAL=1` adds the visual check). Turn the hook on with `git config core.hooksPath .githooks`.

## Deployment

On the server, in SirisAI's `deploy` folder:

```bash
make update
```

From this version, System status can update both apps for you. Run `make auto-update` once on the server to install the updater it uses.

For the spoken morning brief, set in SirisAI's settings: `SIRISAI_SPOKEN_BRIEF_TRIGGER` (e.g. `binary_sensor.kitchen_motion`), `SIRISAI_SPOKEN_BRIEF_PLAYER` (a media_player) and `SIRISAI_SPOKEN_BRIEF_TTS` (e.g. `tts.piper`).
