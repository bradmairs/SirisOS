# SirisOS 12.3.0

**SirisTrails: routes when you zoom in, and a shorter list.**

## The map

- **Zoomed out:** walks close together on screen become one marker with a count (101 around Australia, 35 in New Zealand), coloured orange when they're all hikes. Tap a marker to zoom in on its walks.
- **Zoomed in to city level:** the markers give way to the routes themselves, each the full loop or line walked or hiked. Tap a route to open it. The routes are easy to tap with a finger, and highlight under a mouse.
- **Routes stand out everywhere:** each one has a dark outline, so it's readable along a beach, on water or on satellite. The same goes for the route on a walk's own page.
- **The default view** centres on where you've walked, even when your walks are on both sides of the Pacific, instead of on the Atlantic.
- **Choosing a country or state** scrolls the map into view without the page header covering it.

## The list

**Walks and hikes** shows the latest 5. "Show all 140, by year" opens the rest, grouped by year with the newest first, like the other lists in SirisOS.
