# SirisOS 11.7.0

**SirisGym: one exercise at a time.**

- **A toggle at the top of a workout:** **All exercises** (the scrolling list, as before) or **One at a time**. Your choice is kept on the phone for the next workout.
- **One at a time** shows only the exercise you're on, as a page that fills most of the screen:
  - the exercise's picture, large (tap it for the details);
  - its name, large, with the target ("3 × 5-8") and why;
  - last time, cues and notes;
  - a bigger set table, with bigger boxes and tick buttons to hit between sets.
- **Getting around:**
  - A strip of the workout's exercises sits above the page, each with a little bar showing how many sets are ticked, and a tick once it's done. Tap one to go to it.
  - **Previous** and **Next** buttons under the page name where they go.
  - Swipe the card left or right to turn the page. A swipe that starts on the sets still swipes a set to delete it.
- **Moves on by itself.** When you tick an exercise's last set, the next exercise you haven't finished comes up (after a moment, so you see the tick). In a superset it goes back and forth between the exercises, then on once they're all done. Any you skipped earlier come round again at the end.
- The rest timer, Finish, voice logging and each exercise's menu work the same in both views.

## Update

`make update` on the server, then open SirisOS once so the new version loads. Not yet tried on an iPhone: swiping between exercises was tested in the test suite and the page was checked at phone, tablet and desktop sizes in Chromium.
