# SirisOS 6.1

**Health that runs through the day: training that listens, trends, an early heads-up, and your body in the wrap-up.** Needs SirisAI 3.82.

- **Training listens to the night, not the day.** The training plan's recovery check now uses the HRV your watch took while you slept, so an afternoon workout no longer makes tomorrow look worse. If your energy is under 25, a hard session still to come turns easy. Early signs of getting run down ease the day off too, and likely illness still means rest.
- **The brief shows today's session**, and why it changed if it did ("Changed because: Energy is down to 18 today").
- **This week against your month** on Health → Today. Overnight HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, steps, exercise and more each show this week's average next to your usual, an arrow with the change, and whether that's a good direction for you.
- **An early heads-up.** Two warning signs (temperature, resting heart rate, HRV) on one day give a gentle "early signs you may be getting run down", a day before the full warning. You'll see a banner on Health and a notification.
- **Your body today** in the evening wrap-up shows energy at the start and end of the day against your readiness, and tonight's bedtime for tomorrow's first event.

## Deployment

On the server, in SirisAI's `deploy` folder:

```bash
make update
```
