# SirisOS 3.2

- **Meeting prep** (**Meetings** in the menu, a **Prepare** button on Home during work hours, and every timed event in **Coming up**). Before a meeting it shows:
  - **who's in it**, from the calendar invite;
  - **what Siris knows**: notes matching the meeting's subject and each person's name;
  - **the latest emails from them**, from the last two months, without marking anything read;
  - **related tasks**, which you can tick off right there.
- **Notes after the meeting** save to Siris as "Meeting: <title> (date)", tagged `meeting`. Any line starting with `- [ ]`, `todo` or `action` also becomes a task, with its date read from the words ("…by Friday"). What you type is kept on the device until you save it.
- **A heads-up 10 minutes before** any meeting with other people in it: a phone notification that opens its prep. It goes through your Inbox notifications.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 2.14 or later, which passes on attendees and reads email from them.

```bash
git pull
make up
```
