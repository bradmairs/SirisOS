# SirisOS 7.1

More from the Watch, and SirisGym looks after sore spots, cardio, form videos and your data.

## SirisGym

- **What to lift uses the Watch.** If every set reached the top of the range and your heart rate barely rose, the next session adds two steps instead of one. If you hit the reps but were near your max heart rate and barely recovering between sets, it holds the weight one more session. A grind on the Watch holds the weight even without an RPE. The reason is in the plan, as always.
- **The rest timer learns.** SirisAI now works out how long your heart rate takes to settle after each set (halfway back down from its peak). The rest timer uses the middle of the last three linked workouts for each exercise, between 45 s and 5 min, and the workout says why ("Rest 150 s, not 90"). It can be turned off in Settings → Training.
- **Fitter at the same weight.** An exercise's page compares your peak heart rate at the weight you've lifted most with the Watch on, first time to latest ("At 80 kg your heart rate peaks 11 bpm lower"). SirisAI gets these too.
- **Niggles.** Tell SirisGym something's sore (shoulder, elbow, wrist, lower back or knee). Plans swap the exercises that load it for one that does the same job without, or tell you to go light when nothing does, until you tap Healed. SirisAI knows what's sore.
- **Cardio.** A Cardio button starts a conditioning session; there are more cardio exercises (air bike, ski erg, run, circuit, sled push). The week shows cardio minutes and km, and SirisAI's training summary includes them. Start the Watch with it and the effort analysis works the same way.
- **Form videos.** Tap Film on an exercise during a workout to record the set you're on. Videos are kept on your server with the exercise, set, weight and reps. On the exercise's page, pick two to watch side by side.
- **Backups and export.** Settings lists the copies SirisGym saves before each change it makes on its own. Restore one (your data as it is now is saved first, so a restore can be undone). Download every set as CSV or everything as JSON.
- Recent workouts get the Watch's verdict whenever you open SirisGym or plan a workout, so all of this works without opening each session.

## Getting the most from it

- Start the Watch workout with SirisGym (the start Shortcut), and turn on heart-rate data in Health Auto Export's Workouts automation.
- For set-by-set heart rate, add Heart Rate to the Health Metrics automation with its data not aggregated (each reading). SirisAI uses those readings, every few seconds during a workout, instead of the workout's minute-by-minute heart rate.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 4.39.

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Videos are kept in `gym-videos/` beside your gym file (up to 300 MB each).
