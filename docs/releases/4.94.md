# SirisOS 4.94

- **The tab bar follows your day.** On a phone, the fifth slot of the bottom tab bar changes:
  - **Smart home** when you're home outside work hours;
  - **Career** in work hours (8:30 to 4:30 on weekdays, even if you're working from home), when you're out, or if SirisAI can't tell where you are.

  It uses the same "where and when" as Home, and stays put when Home's "Changes with the time of day" is off. Tab labels now get the room they need, so "Smart home" isn't cut off.
- **The mind map is in the Insights**, full width, after "What the brain noticed" and before "Going quiet".
