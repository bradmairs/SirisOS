# SirisOS 8.9

A round across SirisOS: things that work away from home and without signal, the server watching itself, and backups you can trust.

## Add to SirisOS from anywhere
- **Share Sheet.** Security → Add to SirisOS makes a key (shown once) for an iPhone Shortcut that appears in every app's Share Sheet. A photo or PDF goes to Life admin, where SirisAI files it: a receipt is kept, and what you bought is added to Things you own. A link or text is saved to Siris. Text starting "todo" or "remind me" becomes a task, due today unless it says when.
- The key can only add things, at most 120 an hour; it can't read, change or delete anything. A new key replaces the old one.

## Without signal
- Ticking a task off, adding one on the Tasks screen, and uploading a receipt or letter under Life admin now wait on the phone when SirisOS can't be reached, and go when it's back. Quick Capture, workouts, health logs and the Inbox already did.

## Away from home
- The access check now knows WireGuard: phones on wg-easy (10.8.0.0/24, or SIRISOS_VPN_NETWORKS) show as "your VPN", not "home".

## Return windows
- Receipts that say how long you have to return something (SirisAI 5.94) set a return-by date on each thing. The inbox says so in the last 3 days ("Last day to return the soundbar: tomorrow"). Things you bought in the last 60 days get **Return by** to set or change it.

## The weekly review
- **Your goals:** how many are done, on track and behind the pace for this time of year, with the ones behind listed.
- **Overdue:** tasks past their date, to move or let go. The Sunday push counts them.
- The gym week is part of the review now, not a separate Sunday push.

## The server
- **The server** card (Status): each disk's free space, and whether the local AI's models are all on the GPU. The inbox (and so your phone) is told when a disk is 85% full with under 100 GB left (urgent at 95% or under 10 GB), and when a model is partly in system RAM, with the likely fix.

## Backups
- **A copy off the server.** With SIRISOS_BACKUP_COPY_PATH set to a NAS folder, each nightly backup is copied there too, keeping the newest 14. A copy that fails or goes stale goes to the inbox.
- **A weekly restore check.** The newest backup is opened, and every file checked to be there, the right size, and readable. **Check it now** on the Backups card; a failure goes to the inbox.
