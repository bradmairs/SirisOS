# SirisOS 11.19.0

**Engineering sign-off and three new calculators, container roll-backs, the flight log on Home, and documents kept with renewals.**

These started as ideas for separate apps (a calculation library, a home vault, an update watcher and a drone flight log). Most of each already existed, so the missing parts were added where they belong instead of doubling up. SirisOS stays the hub.

## Engineering

- **Three new calculators** under Pressure pipes, the same on screen and through SirisAI:
  - **Water hammer (surge):** the wave's round trip (2L/a), and whether the closure is rapid. Rapid gives the Joukowsky rise a·Δv/g; slow gives Michaud's 2LΔv/(g·tc). The rise is shown as head and pressure.
  - **Valve flow coefficient (Kv):** the Kv a valve needs to pass a flow at an allowable pressure drop, and its Cv.
  - **Pump duty point:** where the pump curve (through its shut-off head and one rated point) meets the system curve (static head plus friction).
- **Mark as checked:** a saved calculation records who checked it and when, and the PDF sheet's sign-off names them. **Clear** takes it off.

## Home Lab: container updates (with SirisAI 4.32)

- **Recent changes** lists every update, roll-back and failure, including the containers recreated with another because they share its network (wg-easy with gluetun).
- **Roll back:** puts a container back on the version it had before its last update, any time. It's held there, and left out of updates, until you tap **Update now**.
- SirisAI now also checks that the ports a container published still answer after its update, and rolls it back if not.

## SirisDrone on Home

The SirisDrone widget shows **Flights** and **Hours flown** from SirisDrone 1.77's new flight log, plus the last flight (where, when, how long, how high and far), linking to the log.

## Life Admin: documents with renewals

Each renewal (a policy, licence, rego or passport) can keep its document: tap the paperclip to file a photo or PDF with it, and **Open** to see it again. It stays linked when you renew.

New: `POST /api/v1/engineering/calculations/{id}/check`, `GET /api/v1/lab/containers/history`, `POST /api/v1/lab/containers/{name}/roll-back|update`, and `doc_id` on `PATCH /api/v1/renewals/{id}`.
