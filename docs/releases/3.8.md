# SirisOS 3.8

- **Notifications you can act on.** A task that's due comes with **Done** and **In an hour**. An Inbox item comes with its own safe action, such as **Approve**, and **Snooze 3 h** or **Dismiss**. Anything that asks before it acts, like Lock down or sending an email, still opens SirisOS.
- **On iPhone**, notifications can't have buttons, so tapping one opens a page with them, big, one tap away, even when you're signed out. On Android and desktop the buttons are right on the notification, and it updates to say what happened.
- Each notification's buttons work once and stop after two days. They can only act on the one task or item that notification was about.
