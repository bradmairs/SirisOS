# SirisOS 11.16.0

**Ask Siris from anywhere.**

Press the Action Button (or say "Hey Siri, Ask Siris"), speak, and hear SirisAI's answer, at home or away. A question within ten minutes carries on the same conversation.

- **Set it up** on the Security page: **Ask Siris** → **Make a key**, then follow **Make the Shortcut** (Dictate Text → Get Contents of URL to `/api/v1/ask` with the key → Speak Text).
- **The key is its own**, apart from the Add to SirisOS key: shown once, kept only as its SHA-256, replaced by a new one or turned off on the same card. At most 60 questions an hour.
- **What it can do:** ask anything you could ask in the app, and SirisAI can do everyday things (lights, timers). It **can never approve anything**: an action that needs your OK is left in SirisOS for you to confirm.

`POST /api/v1/ask` with `X-SirisOS-Key`: `{text, conversation_id?, new?}` → `{say, conversation_id, link}`.
