# SirisOS 11.16.0

**Apple Pay taps go straight into the budget.**

Some banks (CommBank among them) have no transaction feed for personal customers, so the budget's actual spending only came from a CSV export. Now an iPhone Shortcuts automation sends each Apple Pay purchase to SirisOS the moment you tap.

## How it works

- **Budget → Apple Pay to the budget** makes a key for the Shortcut (shown once). It has the setup steps:
  1. A **Transaction** automation for your cards, set to **Run Immediately**.
  2. **Get Contents of URL** sends the purchase's shop, amount and card, with the key.
  3. Optionally, a notification with SirisOS's reply.
- **Each tap becomes a spend** on the right budget line: a rule you've taught it, or a guess from the shop's name. The reply says where it went: "Logged $12.50 at Woolworths on Groceries."
- **Nothing counts twice.** A tap is kept like a spend added by hand. When a bank CSV later brings the bank's own line for the same purchase (the same amount within three days, the same shop), that line replaces it. The automation firing twice for one purchase is caught too: the same shop and amount within two minutes is one spend.
- The card shows this month's taps, what they came to, and how many still need sorting.
- **The key can only add a spend.** It can't read the budget or anything else. A new key stops the old one; **Turn off** ends it.

Direct debits, BPAY and transfers don't go through Apple Pay, so they still come from the CSV. A tap made with no signal is lost, but it's in the next CSV.

New: `POST /api/v1/budget-tap {merchant, amount, card}` with `X-SirisOS-Key` (no sign-in), and `GET`/`POST`/`DELETE /api/v1/budget/tap-key` (signed in).
