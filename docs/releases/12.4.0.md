# SirisOS 12.4.0

**SirisTrails: the weather on every walk, the routes you repeat, your trips, and the peaks and parks you've been to.**

## Weather

Every walk now shows the weather it was walked in: the sky, the temperature (from start to finish, and how it felt when that was different), rain, wind and gusts, and humidity. It's on the walk's page and in the list.

- Older walks come from Open-Meteo's historical archive; recent ones from its forecast service's past days, because the archive runs about a week behind. Only a rounded start point and the dates are sent.
- New walks get their weather a couple of hours after they finish, in the same background run that names them.
- **Records** add the hottest, coldest, wettest and windiest walks.
- `SIRISOS_TRAILS_WEATHER=off` turns it off. When it's unset, it follows `SIRISOS_TRAILS_PLACES`.

## Routes you repeat

Two walks count as the same route when they're within 15% of each other's length and every part of each runs close to the other. That holds whichever way round you went, and wherever on a loop you started.

- **The "Routes you repeat" card:** each route with how many times you've walked it, your best time, and how the latest compared. Tap a route to see it on the map, with every time as a bar and the fastest in green.
- **On a walk's page:** "Walked 40 times · 6 min off your best", and the other times, each one tap away.
- **In the list:** ↻ and the count.

## Trips

Walks a long way from home (more than 80 km), a few days apart at most and in the same country, make one trip:

- Overseas trips are named by state or country, for example "Tokyo, Japan, Sep 2025".
- Trips at home are named by town, for example "Byron Bay, Nov 2026".

Home is worked out from where most walks started around then, so it moves when you move. Tap a trip to see its walks on the map. A trip in Travel over the same days is linked.

## Peaks and parks

Peaks, parks and tracks get their own list, built from the names OpenStreetMap gave each walk. Each shows how many times you've been and when you were last there, and a tap shows its walks on the map.
