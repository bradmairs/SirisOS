# SirisOS 6.3

**Chartership readiness, well-timed nudges, offline capture, and search that knows the house.**

- **Chartership readiness by element.** Career → Application → "Ready for the interview" now rates every competency element as **none**, **thin** or **solid**, weakest first. Solid means two pieces of evidence, at least one linked to its note or project, and a Part 3 comment of 60 words or more. Each one that isn't solid says what to do next. The weekly review lists the three weakest under "Chartership: next steps".
- **Nudges.** One or two well-timed notifications a day, on a new "Nudges" topic you can turn off in Notifications:
  - When there's a free stretch of 40 minutes or more in the next hour and a home job or task is due ("You've got 55 free minutes, and Clean the dryer filter is due").
  - Half an hour before tonight's suggested bedtime.
  
  There are never more than `SIRISOS_NUDGES_PER_DAY` (2), and none in quiet hours (`SIRISOS_QUIET_HOURS`, 10pm to 7am). `SIRISOS_NUDGES=0` turns them off. Phones that already had every topic get this one too.
- **Offline capture.** If SirisOS can't be reached, a quick capture or a "remind me to…" saved from Home is kept on the phone. It's sent as soon as SirisOS answers again: when you're back online, or the next time the app opens or comes to the front. Photos over about 1.5 MB can't wait this way.
- **Search knows the house.** Search for "heat pump" and you get its home jobs (when each was last done, when it's next due) and the appliance (make, model, warranty).

## Deployment

On the server, in SirisAI's `deploy` folder:

```bash
make update
```
