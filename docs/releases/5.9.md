# SirisOS 5.9

**Readiness that stays put, and an energy score for the day.**

- **Readiness is a morning score.** It comes from last night: your sleep, the HRV your watch took while you slept, your resting heart rate and overnight vitals. Once the night's data is in and it's past 10am (2pm at the latest), it's kept as it was. A workout, a stressful afternoon or a nap no longer moves it. The card says when it has been kept. This needs SirisAI 3.62.
- **Energy** (like Bevel's Body Battery) starts each morning from your readiness and changes through the day. Time awake, workouts and stress (a racing heart while you're sitting still) drain it, and quiet rest and mindful minutes put a little back. It's on the Health screen with its line since waking and what it went on, under Readiness on Home, and in the brief.
- **Health Auto Export setup** (Training → Apple Health) now says to aggregate by **Hours** over the **Previous 7 Days**, and adds heart rate, respiratory rate and mindful minutes. Hourly data is what lets readiness use only your overnight HRV, and lets energy follow your day hour by hour. A sync that only sent the last few hours used to undercount the day.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Update SirisAI to 3.62 too. Then, in Health Auto Export, edit the SirisOS automation: set Aggregate to Hours and Period to Previous 7 Days.
