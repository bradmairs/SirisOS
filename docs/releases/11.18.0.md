# SirisOS 11.18.0

**Tax claims, and SirisGym leaves last time's numbers showing.**

## Tax claims (Budget → Tax)

A new **Tax** tab beside Budget keeps work-related deductions for an Australian tax return, one financial year (1 July to 30 June) at a time.

- **Receipts from email wait to be sorted.** This year's receipts kept from email are listed with a guessed category and the amount from the receipt. Tap **Claim** to check it and add it, or **Not tax** to put it aside.
- **Add one by hand**, with a photo or PDF of the receipt. The file is kept with your other documents and linked to the claim.
- **Each claim has a work share** (a laptop used 80% for work claims 80%). **Work paid me back** makes it claim nothing.
- **The ATO's rules, as you go:**
  - **Under $300:** if your work-related claims come to $300 or less, receipts aren't required, but you need to show how you worked each one out.
  - **Over $300:** every claim needs written evidence, and the screen says which ones don't have it yet. Items of $10 or less, under $200 together, can go in a diary instead.
  - **Tools and equipment:** $300 or less is claimed in full that year. Over $300 is depreciated over its effective life, so it's flagged and left out of the year's total. Items in a set count together.
  - **Working from home:** 70c an hour from your recorded hours. This rate covers power, internet, phone and stationery, so it warns you if you've also claimed those separately.
  - **Car:** 88c a km, up to 5,000 km, and not home to work.
  - **Donations** of $2 or more, and protective or uniform clothing only.
- **Export CSV** for the year (for myTax or your tax agent), and **Receipts (zip)** with every linked receipt.

It's a record-keeper, not tax advice. The rates are the ATO's for 2025-26.

New: `GET /api/v1/tax?fy=`, `POST`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/tax/claims`, `POST /api/v1/tax/not-tax/{doc_id}`, `PUT /api/v1/tax/years/{fy}`, `GET /api/v1/tax/export?fy=`.
