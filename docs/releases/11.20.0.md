# SirisOS 11.20.0

**Standards by topic, and a library of free AU/NZ civil and water standards.**

## Standards by topic

- Engineering → Standards now groups the library by **topic** when you're not searching: A to Z with Other last, and titles in number order, so Books 1 to 9 read in order. A search still lists the best matches first, and topics are searchable.
- Uploading a standard has a **Topic** field that suggests the topics you already use. A new revision keeps its topic.
- **Edit details** (the pencil) corrects a standard's title, authority, reference, edition or topic without uploading it again.

## The library

Free official documents were added, each named and filed by topic:
- **Hydrology and flooding:**
  - Australian Rainfall and Runoff 2019 (v4.2), Books 1–9
  - Melbourne Water's retarding basin design guideline and flood impact (afflux) practice note
- **Stormwater and WSUD:**
  - EPA Victoria's urban stormwater management guidance (Publication 1739)
  - Melbourne Water's MUSIC Guideline 2026, Wetland Design Manual (Parts A2 and C) and Constructed Waterway Design Manual
- **Waterways:** Melbourne Water's waterway corridor and waterway crossing guidelines.
- **Land development and roads:** the Infrastructure Design Manual (v5.6) and its standard drawings.
- **Drinking water:**
  - the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines (v4.0)
  - New Zealand's Drinking Water Quality Assurance Rules (2022, revised 2024)
- **Recycled water:** the Australian Guidelines for Water Recycling (Phase 1, and the stormwater harvesting, managed aquifer recharge and drinking water augmentation modules).
- **Water networks (NZ):** Watercare's Code of Practice Chapter 6, Water.

New: `category` on standards (upload, replace and search), and `PATCH /api/v1/engineering/standards/{id}`.
