# SirisOS 11.12.0

**The budget, split between incomes.**

Budget has a new card at the end, **Split between incomes**. It works out how much each income puts towards the expenses, savings and spending.

## Choose who shares it

- **All** the incomes, or tap to choose some: two pays, say, without an allowance.
- **How to split:**
  - **By income** (the default): everyone puts in the same share of what they earn, so a bigger pay puts in more;
  - **Equally**: everyone puts in the same amount;
  - **Whole lines**: each line goes to one income (one pays the electricity, the other the rent), kept as close to by-income as whole lines allow. Fewer transfers.
- **Personal lines** go to their owner, whatever the method. A line naming one income's owner is suggested: with an income called "Sam's Pay", lines called "Sam", "Gym - Sam" and "Phone - Sam" go to it. Under **Personal lines**, any line can be given to someone, or shared. What someone pays personally still counts towards their share.

## What each income puts in

For each income:

- the amount per its own pay (per fortnight, for a fortnightly pay), and per month;
- the share of its income that is, against its share of the budget and of the income;
- what's left, or how much it's over;
- the split across expenses, savings and spending, with a bar;
- tap it for each line, marked personal or a share.

If the chosen incomes don't cover the budget, it says by how much, and how much of that the left-out incomes would cover.

## Keep it, and redo it

**Keep this split** saves the choices and the result. When the budget changes after that, the card says what changed ("Rent up from $2,000 to $2,100 a month"), and **Redo the split** works it out again with the same choices. Changing the choices shows the new split straight away; **Back to the kept one** undoes that.

New: `GET /api/v1/budget/split`, `POST /api/v1/budget/split/preview` and `PUT /api/v1/budget/split`, each taking `{sources, method, personal}`. The split is kept in `budget-split.json`, so saving the budget never touches it.
