# SirisOS 7.31

## Work

- **No calendar.** Work no longer shows meetings or a next meeting, and doesn't ask SirisAI for the calendar. Your work calendar isn't linked, and you don't want it. The At work card on Home drops the next meeting too.
- **No projects.** The Work projects card is gone: Projects is for your personal projects. To-dos in your work notes (GVW and other Work/ notes) are still listed.

## Deployment

git pull && make up
