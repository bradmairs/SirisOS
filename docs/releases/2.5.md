# SirisOS 2.5

- **System status:** a new screen (sidebar → **System status**, or "System status →" under Siris apps on Home) showing every connected app's health over time, not just right now: whether it's up, its uptime over the last 24 hours and 7 days, its average response time, a strip of the last 24 hours (green, amber, red), and its recent outages with when they started, how long they lasted and why.
- SirisOS checks every app every 5 minutes in the background (and whenever SirisOS is open), so the history has no gaps overnight. Seven days are kept.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull
make up
```

History is kept in `data/app/status-history.json`. `SIRISOS_STATUS_SAMPLER=0` turns the background checks off.
