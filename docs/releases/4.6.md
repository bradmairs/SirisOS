# SirisOS 4.6

**Your Chartered application, in Career.**

Career has a new **Application** tab for Engineers Australia's three forms for the Chartered interview. You can work through each one here, and keep your filled-in copy with it.

- **Self-reflection on the chartered competencies** (v4.1):
  - The area of practice you're seeking.
  - **Part 1:** 19 questions, answered Yes or Not sure. Anything you mark Not sure goes on a "to prepare" list, with the elements it relates to.
  - **Part 2:** four Yes or No questions.
  - **Part 3:** a short comment on each of the 16 elements, citing one recent work episode. "Start from my evidence" drafts one from the latest evidence you've tagged to that element.
  - **Part 4:** the signed statement.
- **CPD questionnaire** (v2.0). Choose how you'll show your CPD: send your Engineers Australia CPD log, or answer the 16 questions. "Fill from my CPD" starts an answer from your imported CPD for the last three years, with hours. Applying needs no minimum hours; once you're Chartered it's 150 hours every three years.
- **Building Industry Assessment** (VIC, WA, ACT). Say whether you need one. If you do, it lists what each state asks for: for Victoria's Building Industry Endorsement, a CV, building-industry CPD, and a statement of three years' experience with the Building Act 1993, the Building Regulations 2018 and the NCC.
- **Ready for the interview.** An eight-item checklist with a percentage. It appears:
  - at the top of the tab
  - on the Career overview
  - in Career's next steps, which now lead with the application
- **Goals.** On Your year, a new goal type, "Chartered application ready", follows that percentage.
- **Pathways.** The Chartered pathway gains three steps after the self-assessment: the self-reflection questionnaire, CPD evidence, and the Building Industry Assessment. Existing career files get them once; delete one and it stays deleted.

The blank forms are in Siris (CPEng Chartership, vault 1.64).

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Filled-in copies are kept in `career-documents/`, next to `career.json` in the data folder, so backups include them.
