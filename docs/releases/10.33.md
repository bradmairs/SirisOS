# SirisOS 10.33

- **The Friday debrief, only on Friday** (with SirisAI 7.29). The debrief card on Home now shows on Friday only. Before, it stayed up through the weekend until you answered it.
  - **Add another:** after you save one, you can add another (for a different project, say) or tap Done. Otherwise it doesn't come back that week.
  - **Nothing this week:** puts it away until next Friday without filing anything.

To update: `make update`.
