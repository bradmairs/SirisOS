# SirisOS 10.2

- **Meals, with Mealie.** A new **Meals** page plans the week's dinners.
  - **From your Mealie recipes:** search them, or tap a dinner you have often. With Mealie connected, the plan *is* Mealie's meal plan (dinners), so planning in either shows in both, and each dinner links to its recipe in Mealie.
  - **Without Mealie** (or for leftovers): type the dinner and, if you like, what to buy for it.
  - **Shopping for the week:** the week's ingredients, with the same thing from two recipes made into one line (e.g. onions from two dinners). It leaves out things you always have (salt, pepper, oil; you can change these) and anything already on your Home Assistant shopping list. Tick what you need and add it to the list.
  - **Dinner tonight** shows on Home from midday.
  - **Setting up Mealie:** set `SIRISOS_MEALIE_URL` and `SIRISOS_MEALIE_TOKEN` (a Mealie API token: your profile, API Tokens). Mealie 1 and 2 both work.
- **Notification roundups.** Notifications that can wait now come together in a morning (7:30) and evening (18:00) roundup. A roundup with one thing in it is just that notification.
  - **Still straight away:** urgent alerts, tasks at their time, the Friday timesheet reminder, money getting tight, bedtime, and the out-of-office reminder.
  - **Change the times or turn it off** in Notifications.
  - **Not covered:** SirisAI's own alerts that go through ntfy.
- **Trips.** A new **Trips** page. Add where and when, and one card brings it together:
  - **the weather there** for each day (once it's within 16 days);
  - **a packing list** made from the length, the weather and whether it's overseas, to tick off and add to;
  - **bills and renewals due while you're away**;
  - **the out-of-office:** a reminder at 3pm on your last workday before you go, until you tick it off;
  - **away mode:** a button to run your SirisAI away protocol.

  The card shows on Home from a week before you go until you're back.
- **A better summary for your doctor** (with SirisAI 7.28). Health's **Summary for a doctor** now adds:
  - **Worth mentioning:** what's going the wrong way over the last month (sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, weight, activity, mood, blood oxygen, drinks a week);
  - **each month side by side;**
  - **one A4 page** when printed. Print it or save it as a PDF from the page that opens.
- **Last month in short.** In the first week of each month, a card on Home sums up last month: money (spent against the plan, net worth), health (sleep, heart, steps and mood against the month before), timesheet and CPD hours, and how often you kept each habit. It's also filed in Siris as "<Month> recap", and a notification says when it's ready. SirisGym sessions aren't included.

To update: `make update`. To use Mealie, add the settings above to `.env` first.
