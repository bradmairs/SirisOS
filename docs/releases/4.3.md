# SirisOS 4.3

- **Talk to SirisOS.** Tap the microphone in the top bar and say what you want. It stops listening when you stop talking, does it, and says what it did.
  - **"Add a task to call the plumber Friday"** or "remind me to put the bins out tonight" adds the task, with its day and time.
  - **"What's on tomorrow?"** reads you the day from the calendar. "What's on Friday?" works too.
  - **"Log 1.5 hours CPD for the stormwater webinar"** adds a reminder to log it in Engineers Australia tomorrow, like the wrap-up does.
  - **"Note: the Werribee levels are in the shared drive"** saves it to Siris.
  - **Anything else** goes to SirisAI, which answers in a sentence or two.
- You can type instead. The speaker button turns spoken replies off. **Open** takes you to the task, the week or wherever the answer lives.
- **A Home Screen shortcut:** on **iPhone shortcuts** there's now a `/talk` link that opens SirisOS listening.

## Deployment

Speech-to-text goes through SirisAI, as voice memos already do. The microphone needs SirisOS opened over HTTPS.
