# SirisOS 10.31

- **The tab bar's bubble lines up and swells.** Fixes from your iPhone recording:
  - **In the right place:** the bubble now sits exactly on the tab you're on. Before, it could sit between two tabs and be narrower than them, because it was measured once before the bar had settled into its final width. It's now measured again whenever the bar changes size.
  - **Swells while you drag:** it grows into a bigger, clearer drop of glass that stands out past the bar, and the icon under your finger grows a little. Before, its pop-in animation held it at normal size.
  - **Smooth:** it follows your finger every frame without redrawing the whole bar, then springs onto the tab you let go on. Letting go on the tab you're already on springs it back.

To update: `make update`.
