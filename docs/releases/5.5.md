# SirisOS 5.5

**Training for your next race, and Apple Health on its own.**

## Training

A new **Training** screen (in the sidebar, and from the Fitness card) builds a plan for your next race.

- **Add a race:** its name, the day, the distance (5 km, 10 km, half or full marathon) and, if you like, a goal time.
- **The plan starts from what you've actually been running:** the average of your last four weeks from Apple Health, or a weekly figure you give it.
  - It builds about 10% a week, with an easier week every fourth week, towards a peak that suits the distance. It then tapers before race day.
  - Each week has three runs, or four from 30 km a week: an easy run on Tuesday, a tempo run on Thursday, the long run on Saturday, and an easy Sunday. The race takes its own day.
- **Today, adjusted for how you've recovered.** SirisOS compares last night's sleep, HRV and resting heart rate with your own last 30 days.
  - One of them off: a hard session becomes an easy one ("Easy run, 5 km instead").
  - Two or more off: it says to rest.
- **In your calendar.** Subscribe to the plan on your iPhone and each run sits next to work. It updates as the plan changes, and you can make a new link at any time to stop the old one.
- **On Home.** A **Today's run** card shows in the morning, in the evening and at weekends, while you have a race.
- **The Fitness card's weekly goal** follows the plan while you have a race.

## Apple Health, on its own

Apple Health has no internet connection of its own, so the data has to come from the phone. The Training page sets this up:

1. **Make a key** on the Training page.
2. In the **Health Auto Export** iPhone app, add a **REST API** automation:
   - **URL:** the address shown on the page.
   - **Header:** `Authorization: Bearer <your key>`.
   - **Settings:** JSON, aggregated by day.
   - **Data:** steps, active energy, exercise, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, weight and workouts.
3. Turn on automatic sync.

From then on, your health data reaches SirisOS whenever the phone is unlocked, and SirisAI stores it. The brief, Fitness, Your year and Training all read it from there. A personal Shortcuts automation posting to the same address works too.

Only a fingerprint of the key is kept. Making a new key stops the old one.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

This needs **SirisAI 3.11** or later, which reads Health Auto Export's format. For the phone to reach the import address from outside the house, use the same address you use for SirisOS away from home (System status → Access from here).
