# SirisOS 11.4.0

**SirisOS works offline, so a flight doesn't stop you.**

- **Get ready for offline.** In the menu, under More, **Offline** opens a sheet with a **Get ready for offline** button. On Wi-Fi with SirisOS open, it loads every screen's code and data and keeps the 30 most recently updated Siris notes on the phone, then says what's ready.
  - **It does it by itself the evening before a flight:** when Trips has a trip today or tomorrow, or your calendar has a flight in the next 24 hours, and it hasn't got ready in the last 12 hours.
- **Every screen keeps what it last showed.** Offline, a screen opens with its last data, and the sync bar says how old it is ("Offline · showing what it had at Sun 9:40 pm").
- **Everyday changes wait and sync.** Offline, changes to tasks, habits, the journal and mood, health logs, the wrap-up, the timesheet and focus, meals, budget sorting and balances, SirisGym, Siris notes, goals, CPD, Around the Week, trips, the weekly review and Home's layout are kept on the phone and sent in order when you're back. A habit you tick shows ticked straight away.
  - **Never queued:** home controls, cameras, approvals, the Terminal and commands. These still need a connection, so nothing acts on the house hours later.
- **More room on the phone.** What SirisOS keeps now lives in the browser's database (IndexedDB) instead of its small key store. It asks the phone to keep it, and moves what was already kept the first time it opens. Bigger photos and documents can wait offline now (12 MB and 20 MB).
- **A sync bar.** It shows "Offline · 2 waiting", then "Synced 2 changes", and "1 change didn't sync · Review" when SirisOS said no to something. Tap it for the Offline sheet:
  - what's waiting, with **Let go** and **Sync now**;
  - what didn't go and why, with **Retry** and **Let go**.
- **Safe merging.**
  - Each change keeps the time it was made and is sent once.
  - If a Siris note you edited offline changed on the server meanwhile, both are kept: yours is saved beside it as "<title> (edited offline <date>)".
- **Ask SirisAI offline.** A question asked with no connection waits. When you're back, SirisOS asks it in the background and the answer comes as a notification that opens the conversation. If the answer wants to do something, it asks you to open SirisAI and confirm: nothing is done on its own.
- **Today, offline.** After getting ready, Home's plan for the day, tasks, calendar, this week's dinners and your trip are all there with no connection, as of when it got ready.

New: `POST /api/v1/assistant/ask-later`, `GET /api/v1/assistant/asked`, `GET /api/v1/brain/recent`.

## Not yet tried on an iPhone

Tested in the test suite and in Chromium, not yet on an iPhone:

- **Moving to IndexedDB:** the first open after updating moves what was kept. If it takes over 1.5 seconds, SirisOS opens with what it has.
- **Get ready for offline:** it loads each screen in a hidden frame. Keep SirisOS open on the screen until it says it's ready; it takes about a minute and a half.
- **No background sync on iOS:** changes sync when SirisOS is open and back online, not while it's closed.

## Update

`make update` on the server, then open SirisOS on the phone once on Wi-Fi so the new version loads. Then tap **Get ready for offline** before the flight.
