# SirisOS 4.91

**Make and change protocols.**

- **Protocols on Smart home.** A new Protocols card, under the home controls, lists every protocol. Each shows what it does and whether it's built-in or your version of one.
- **Edit any of them.** Tap the pencil to change what it's for and its steps:
  - add, remove and reorder steps;
  - for each step, pick what it does: a home device (lights, switches, fans, TV and media, heating and cooling, blinds and garage doors, locks, scenes, scripts), a notification, or guest mode;
  - pick when it runs: always, only when nobody's home, only when someone's home, only at night, or only in the day.

  Saving a built-in (Lock down, Away, Guests over, Guests gone) makes it your version, and **Back to built-in** restores the original.
- **New protocol** makes your own, such as a "Movie night" that dims the lounge lamp and closes the blind at night.
- **It shows up everywhere:** what you save is what Home's Protocols card runs, and what Siris uses in chat.
- **Steps made in chat** that this editor can't make (another kind of tool, its own undo, or a condition on a device's state) are kept as they are when you edit the rest. Ask Siris in chat to change those.

Running a protocol still always asks first, and can be undone.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

Needs **SirisAI 2.91**.
