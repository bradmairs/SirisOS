# SirisOS 9.42

- **Lights show what they really are.** Home controls could show a light as on when it was off, and off when it was on. There were two causes:
  - **What a tap said stood for good.** After you tapped a light, SirisOS kept what SirisAI answered, even when later reads said otherwise, so a light changed in the Home app, by a protocol or at the switch kept showing what SirisOS last set. Now every fresh read of the controls replaces it.
  - **The answer could be the old state.** SirisAI read the light back the moment Home Assistant took the tap, before a Zigbee, Hue or HomeKit light had switched. SirisAI 6.6 now waits for it. If it still hasn't switched after 3 seconds, SirisAI says nothing rather than give the old state, and SirisOS reads the controls again 2.5 seconds later.

Needs SirisAI 6.6 for the second part.
