# SirisOS 5.7

**Health: readiness, sleep, fitness and vitals, from Apple Health.**

A new **Health** section in the sidebar, in the style of Bevel. Every score is measured against your own recent normal, not a population average.

- **Today: readiness, 0 to 100.** How ready your body is to take on strain today.
  - What goes in:
    - HRV against your last 30 days (35%)
    - resting heart rate against your usual (20%)
    - last night's sleep against what you need (30%)
    - vitals (10%): breathing rate, wrist temperature and blood oxygen, which flag when you might be getting sick
    - yesterday's load (5%)
  - 70 and over is **Primed**, 45 to 69 **Steady**, and under 45 **Recover**. Each comes with plain advice and the contributors that drove it, weakest first.
  - Until there's a week of data it's marked as early.
- **Sleep.**
  - Last night: hours, sleep score, stages (deep, REM, core, awake) and when you fell asleep and woke.
  - Two weeks of nights against your need.
  - Your 7-night average, sleep debt this week, and how regular your bedtime is.
- **Fitness.**
  - Training load: exercise this week against the last four, with 0.8 to 1.3 as the sweet spot.
  - 28 days of exercise, two weeks of steps, and your workouts with time, distance, energy and heart rate.
- **Vitals.** HRV, resting and walking heart rate, breathing rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, VO₂ max, weight and body fat. Each shows its latest value, your usual range shaded behind 30 days, and a flag when it's outside that range.
- **In the morning brief.** It now leads with your readiness ("Readiness 82, primed: a good day to push…") and has a Readiness card with the ring and the top contributors. Home shows the same card in the morning and at weekends.
- **Training.** The plan now listens to readiness too: a Recover day eases the session, and a very low score means rest.

All of this comes from Health Auto Export. SirisOS now keeps everything it sends, not only the eight metrics SirisAI keeps. Older history from SirisAI fills in behind it.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

In Health Auto Export, keep **All Selected** (or at least sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, VO₂ max, steps, active energy, exercise) and add a second automation for **Workouts** if you want them listed. The data is kept in `health-days.json` in the data folder, so backups include it.
