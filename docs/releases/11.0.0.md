# SirisOS 11.0.0

_Was 10.4 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a section)._

**Around the week.** Cards on Home that turn up when they're useful, two new Home Lab cards, and a new **Around the Week** screen in the menu to set them up.

- **Fuel on the way to Shepparton.** It finds the cheapest Unleaded 91 in the first 30 km of the Wednesday 6am drive, within 1.5 km of the road.
  - **When it shows:** the card is on Home from Tuesday 6pm until Wednesday 9am, with a notification 30 minutes before you leave.
  - **What you see:** the best servo, how far in it is, how much it saves against the others on the way, and a Map link for each.
  - **Settings:** the day, time, fuel and distance are all on Around the Week.
  - **Data:** prices come from Service Victoria's Fair Fuel open data and are about a day old. It needs a free key in `SIRISOS_FAIR_FUEL_KEY`.
  - **Route:** the public OSRM router works out the road from where SirisAI's weather is set. The route is kept between checks.
- **This weekend.** From Thursday to Sunday, Home shows the weekend's best dry, free daylight windows, checked against the hourly forecast and your calendar. A notification comes with Thursday's 6pm roundup.
  - **Routes:** add your usual routes and each window gets the longest one that fits.
  - **Recovery:** when readiness says recover, it suggests a walk instead.
- **Watch tonight.** From 6pm, it suggests what fits before bed from Plex: carry on with what you're watching, a film if there's time, then something new. It also lists the episodes and films coming this week from Sonarr and Radarr.
- **Bin night.** Add each bin and how often it's collected. The card shows from the evening before until 9am, with a notification at 7pm.
- **Bedroom before bed.** Pick the Home Assistant temperature sensor, and humidity if you have one.
  - **From an hour before bed:** the bedroom's reading, and whether to cool it or warm it.
  - **After two weeks:** the temperature range you sleep best at.
- **Garden and rain.** Each morning it says whether to water, based on rain in the last three days and the next two, and the heat. **Water now** runs your watering protocol, and **Done** marks it watered.
- **Catch-ups.** From Friday to Sunday, Home lists the people you're overdue to catch up with (from their keep-in-touch gap) and the free parts of the weekend. **Caught up** resets the clock.
- **Home Lab: container updates.** Lists containers with a newer image, with **Update**, **Update all** and **Check now**. SirisAI does the updating.
- **Home Lab: internet speed.** A chart of each day's typical download speed against your plan, plus dropouts. A notification comes after three slow days.

Needs SirisAI 4.22.0 or later, for the hourly forecast, watching, containers, internet and sensors hub endpoints.

Not yet tried against the real services:
- the Fair Fuel feed, with a real key and its real station locations
- the OSRM route
- Plex, Sonarr and Radarr data
- your Home Assistant sensor names
- the 5:30am Wednesday notification
- the cards on iPhone

To update: add `SIRISOS_FAIR_FUEL_KEY` to `.env` if you want fuel prices, then `make update`.
