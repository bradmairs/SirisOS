# SirisOS 7.91

- **SirisGym gets your weight from Apple Health reliably.** It used to read weight from SirisAI's health overview, which works out 70 days of scores first (about 2 s, sometimes more). When that took longer than the gym waits (6 s), SirisGym got no weight at all, and a weigh-in older than 70 days never counted. Now weight comes straight from the daily health records, up to 400 days back, alongside the overview. That feeds the protein target, the strength ratios and the body chart. Readiness still comes from the overview.
- **Health → Data sync shows weight.** It shows the last weight that came through (kg and date). If none has, it says to tick Weight & Body Mass in Health Auto Export's Health Metrics automation, and it lists any weight-like metric sent under a name SirisAI doesn't use.
