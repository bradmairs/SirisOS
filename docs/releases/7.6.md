# SirisOS 7.6

- **Handwriting to check.** Siris (the brain) has over 300 handwritten OneNote pages read by the vision model, and some readings are wrong ("Dellers underslip rear Meroh"). A new card at the top of the brain's Manage section shows one page at a time:
  - the page itself, drawn from its ink in OneNote, beside the machine's reading;
  - the words that look misread (ones no other note uses), as chips;
  - **It's right** to mark it checked, or edit the reading and **Save my reading** to replace it with yours;
  - **Skip** to leave it for later.

  The likeliest misreadings come first. Needs SirisAI 4.9 and the Siris engine 2.28; with an older SirisAI the card doesn't show.
- New API routes, passed through to SirisAI: `GET /api/v1/brain/handwriting`, `GET /api/v1/brain/handwriting/image` (a PNG, with `X-Page-Parts`) and `POST /api/v1/brain/handwriting/check`.
