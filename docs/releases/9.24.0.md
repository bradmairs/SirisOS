# SirisOS 9.24.0

_Was 10.0 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

- **Actual spending against the budget.** Budget has a new **Spending** card. Add your bank's CSV export (from its website or app) and see the month's real spending against each line's plan.
  - **Any bank's export:** with or without a header row (CommBank's has none), one amount column or debit and credit, dates as 19/11/2026 or 2026-11-19. Adding the same file twice adds nothing twice.
  - **Sorted for you:** each spend goes on a line, from a guess at the shop's name (Woolworths, Coles and Aldi on Groceries; BP, Shell and Ampol on Fuel...). Money in, such as pay and refunds, is left out.
  - **It learns:** put one spend on a line, or leave it out, and every other one from that shop follows, now and in later imports. Guesses are listed so you can check them.
  - **The month:** each line shows what's spent of its plan, marks lines that are over, and warns about lines running ahead of the month. You can look back at earlier months.
  - **The weekly review** lists any lines over plan and spends still to sort, and its Sunday notification says how many lines are over.
- **Balances, goals and net worth.** Budget has a new **Balances** card.
  - **Accounts:** everyday, savings, super, investments, loans and credit cards, each with its balance. Update them now and then; a bank CSV with a balance column updates its account by itself.
  - **Real goal progress:** tie a savings account to a savings goal. The goal then shows the real balance and a forecast of the month you'll reach it, from how fast the balance grew over the last three months.
  - **Net worth:** everything you have less what you owe, with a line showing it month by month.
- **Timesheet notes fill themselves in.** Name a focus block in Work ("What's this block for?", with an optional code). When it ends, its time goes on the timesheet, added to that day's entry if there is one. On Friday from 3pm a notification gives the week's hours and the days that look light (under 6 hours), ready to copy into the real timesheet.
- **Home Assistant lists in Tasks** (with SirisAI 4.17.0). Your Home Assistant to-do lists, such as the shopping list, show in Tasks. Tick items off, add new ones, or show the done ones to untick.
- **A sun and heat heads-up** (with SirisAI 4.17.0). On days with UV 6 or more, the morning focus card and the brief give the UV and the hours you need sun protection, e.g. "UV 11 (extreme) 9am–4pm: sunscreen, hat and shade". On days of 32° or hotter they also remind you to drink water and keep out of the afternoon sun.

To update: `make update`.
