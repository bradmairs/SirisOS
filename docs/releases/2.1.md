# SirisOS 2.1

- **This week:** a new screen (sidebar → **This week**, or "This week →" under Coming up on Home) with Monday to Sunday at a glance. Each day shows its events (multi-day all-day events on every day they cover), the forecast (high / low, and rain when it's likely), and what's due: Second Brain project deadlines and your career goals. Anything overdue in the Second Brain sits at the top. Calendar events that look like CPD are marked with their hours, and the week's CPD adds up what's in your Engineers Australia import plus what's on the calendar. Use the arrows for other weeks.
- Search finds "This week" too, and the week is kept on the device like Home, so it opens with no signal.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 1.21 or later for the calendar and forecast.

```bash
git pull
make up
```
