# SirisOS 7.7

A budget tool, things you own and people, Today on Home, and doing more without a connection. **Things & people and the timeline's bills, birthdays and trips need SirisAI 5.0**; the budget is SirisOS's own and works on its own.

## Budget (new screen, in the menu)

- **Income**, then three sections: **Expenses** (bills and fixed costs), **Savings** (with a goal and what's saved so far) and **Spending** (the day-to-day). Each line has an amount and how often (a week, a fortnight, a month, a quarter, a year). Tap a suggestion (Rent or mortgage, Groceries, Holiday…) to add a line.
- **Show per** week, fortnight, month or year: every figure and chart follows.
- **Totals**: income, each section with its share of income, your savings rate, and what's left over (green), or by how much you're over (red, with a warning).
- **Charts** (hover or tab to any part for the figures):
  - **Where it goes**: a ring of expenses, savings, spending and what's left over, with the amount left in the middle.
  - **All of your income**: one bar split the same way; past your income, a line marks money you don't have.
  - **Against the 50/30/20 guide**: needs, wants and savings as shares of income, each against its guide.
  - **Biggest lines**: your largest costs, ranked.
  - **Savings over the next year**: what you'll have put by month by month, with when each goal is reached, and a progress bar per goal.
  - **Your plan over time**: how income and each section have changed, month by month (from next month).
- **Show as a table**: every line, its section, its amount per period and its share of income.
- **Add subscriptions and bills from email**: brings in the renewals SirisAI found in your email as expense lines.
- Saved as you type. Without a connection, changes stay on the device and are sent when SirisOS is back.

## Things & people (new screen, in the menu)

- **Things you own**: what, where, when bought and for how much, and the warranty, with a warning when it ends within a month and a link to the manual. Receipts added under Life admin fill this in.
- **People**: birthdays and anniversaries, soonest first, with how old they're turning. The gift button shows gift ideas from your notes and when you last caught up, and has **Caught up today**.

## Today (Home)

A new **Today** widget at the top of Home's Today section:
- what's on today in time order (the calendar and timed tasks), with what's past greyed out;
- **Any time today**: all-day events, and tasks due or overdue;
- **Coming up**: bills due in the next three days, birthdays this week, and a trip countdown.

It can be moved or hidden like any widget.

## Without a connection

Health logs (water, mood, a symptom) and **Got it** or **dismiss** in the Inbox now wait on the phone and go when SirisOS is back, like captures and tasks already did. Approvals never wait: by the time they'd send, things may have changed.

## Behind it

- `GET/PUT /api/v1/budget` keeps the budget in `budget.json` (nightly backups include it) with a month-by-month history. `GET /api/v1/budget/from-bills` turns SirisAI's bills into expense lines.
- `/api/v1/life/things`, `/people`, `/people/{id}/gifts`, `/news`, `/trial` and `/reliability` pass through to SirisAI 5.0.
- The charts are drawn by SirisOS itself (`components/Charts.tsx`), with no chart library. Their colours were checked for colour-blind separation and contrast in both themes. Each chart has hover and keyboard tooltips and labels its values directly.
- The visual check now covers Budget and Things & people at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
