# SirisOS 11.1.0

_Was 10.5 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

**A shorter side menu.** It goes from 26 entries to 13, under the headings Daily, You, Home & life, Work, Siris and Server. Screens that belong together now share one entry, with tabs across the top to move between them:

- **Tasks:** Tasks and Projects.
- **Reviews:** Tonight (Wrap Up), This week (Weekly Review) and This year (Your Year).
- **Health:** Health and Fitness.
- **Household:** Around the week, Meals and Smart Home.
- **Life Admin:** Life Admin, Trips, and Things & People.
- **Work:** Work, Engineering and Career.
- **Server:** Status, Home Lab, Terminal and Security.

Home, SirisGym, Budget, SirisAI, Siris and Links stay as they were.

- **It remembers your tab.** A menu entry opens the tab you last used in it on that device.
- **Nothing moves.** Every screen keeps its address, so notification links, Shortcuts and bookmarks still work.
- **Search still finds each screen by its old name, and by its group's name too.** For example, "Server" finds Home Lab.
- **The phone tab bar is unchanged.**

To update: `make update`.
