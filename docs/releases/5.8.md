# SirisOS 5.8

**Health in one place, with insights.**

SirisAI 3.51 now keeps all your health data and works out the scores. SirisOS shows them, so the Health page, chat and voice ("how ready am I?") always agree.

## Your data, in SirisAI

- **One store.** Health Auto Export's data goes straight to SirisAI. It keeps every metric, your sleep times and stages, and each workout.
  - SirisOS no longer keeps its own copy, or the two readers it had for the export.
  - On first use, your earlier history moves across from `health-days.json`. The file is then renamed `health-days.migrated.json` and kept as a backup.
- **Years of history.** Health → Data:
  - In the Health app, tap your picture, then Export All Health Data.
  - Choose the `export.zip` on the Data tab.
  - Your phone and watch's steps aren't counted twice. Days Health Auto Export already sent keep its values.
- **When the import stops.** If nothing arrives for 36 hours, the Inbox says so. The Training page still shows what the phone sent and why it failed.
- **Export and delete.** Health → Data:
  - a CSV of everything;
  - a printable summary for a doctor (print it to save a PDF);
  - deleting a date range.

## New on the Health page

- **Getting sick?** When temperature, resting heart rate and HRV point the same way two days running, a warning shows at the top, and the training plan rests that day.
- **Tonight.** The bedtime to aim for, from what you need, this week's sleep debt and tomorrow's first event.
- **Weight.** A smoothed trend rather than the daily up and down: kg a week, and weeks to your goal.
- **What your sleep goes with**, from your own nights: late meetings, late workouts, caffeine, drinks, a warm bedroom, late TV.
- **Goals** (Fitness tab): "1,000 km run by 31 December", with progress and whether you're on pace.
- **Log** tab:
  - water, coffee, drinks and mood in a tap;
  - symptoms, supplements and medication as a note;
  - a meal photo for a rough calorie and protein estimate.
  - You can also log these by voice.

## Training

- **Follows what you did.** Each run in the plan shows done (✓), partly or missed, from your workouts. A tempo or long run missed in the last two days moves to today rather than piling onto next week.
- **Race prediction** from your best recent run.
- **Paces** for easy, long and tempo runs.
- **Heart-rate zones** from the highest heart rate your workouts have recorded.
- **Strength and mobility days**: mobility on Monday and strength on Wednesday, left out in race week. A low-readiness day turns strength into mobility.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```

This needs **SirisAI 3.51** or later, which keeps the health data and works out the scores. Update SirisAI first; until then, the Health page shows no data.
