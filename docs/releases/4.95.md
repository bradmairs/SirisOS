# SirisOS 4.95

**The tab bar does more with its last two buttons.**

- **The fourth button follows the day:**
  - **Brief** in the morning;
  - **Wrap up** from wind down (8 pm) and through the night;
  - **Links** the rest of the time.
- **The fifth follows where you are:**
  - **Career** in work hours;
  - **Smart home** when you're home;
  - **Tasks** when you're out;
  - **Career** when SirisAI can't tell.
- **Badges.** A dot on **Siris** when the brain has notes waiting to be sorted, or a brain item is open in your inbox. A dot on **Smart home** when the house needs you: an intruder or presence alert, Home Assistant, a camera, a protocol.
- **Choose them yourself.** Times of day now has a **Tab bar** row for each part of the day. Leave each button on Automatic, or pick Links, Career, Smart home, Tasks, Wrap up, Today's brief, Projects, Engineering, Weekly review, Your year or System status. Home, SirisAI and Siris always stay first.

With "Changes with the time of day" off, it's Links and Career all day.
