# SirisOS 10.1

- **Until pay day.** Budget has a new card that runs your everyday balance forward, day by day, to just after your next pay. It starts from today's balance, takes off your usual day-to-day spending (from your bank imports, or your budget's spending lines until there's three weeks of them) and the recurring charges as they fall due, and adds pay as it comes in.
  - **The lowest point** is what to watch: e.g. "Lowest point $169 on Thu 26 Nov".
  - **Under $200** (or `SIRISOS_CASHFLOW_LOW`): Home shows a **Money before pay** card, and you get one notification at 9am for each low point.
  - **Needs** your everyday account's balance in Balances, or a bank CSV with a balance column.
- **Recurring charges.** SirisOS finds the bills and subscriptions in your bank imports: the same place, about the same amount, at a steady pace (weekly to yearly). Each one shows when it's next due, and it's flagged when:
  - **the price went up** since last time (with what it was);
  - **it isn't in your budget**;
  - **it's a subscription you haven't said you're keeping** ("still using it?").

  Mark each one **Keep it**, **Cancelling** or **Not a bill**, or undo that.
- **Suggestions for your plan.** After two full months of bank imports, Budget suggests new amounts for lines that usually run well over (or under) their plan, with where the money went, e.g. "Groceries averages $1,104 a month against $866.67 planned. Mostly Woolworths and Aldi." One tap updates the line. Lines with nothing sorted onto them are left alone, as are yearly and quarterly ones.

To update: `make update`.
