# SirisOS 1.8

- **CPD pacing:** Career → CPD has a new **Next 12 months** card. Engineers Australia counts a rolling 3 years, so hours older than two years will drop out within a year. The card works out what you need in the next 12 months to stay at 150 hours and keep each minimum (area of practice, risk management, business and management), about how much that is a month, and whether your last 12 months are keeping that pace: **On track** or **Behind**.
- **Where to catch up:** for each category that's behind, what usually counts (for example a HAZOP or safety-in-design course for risk) and, when SirisLearn is connected, matching SirisLearn courses.
