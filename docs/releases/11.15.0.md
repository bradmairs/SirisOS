# SirisOS 11.15.0

**A wall display, undo instead of "Are you sure?", a fuller monthly recap, and a privacy check on every change.**

## A wall display

For a spare tablet in the kitchen. It shows, in big type:

- the time and date;
- today's weather;
- today's events, and tomorrow's in a line;
- tonight's dinner;
- bin night;
- parcels on the way.

It keeps the screen on and checks again every minute. From wind-down to the morning it dims (Times of day), and a tap brightens it for a minute.

**The tablet never signs in.** Security → **Wall display** makes an address with its own key (shown once). Open it on the tablet and add it to the Home Screen. The key reads only the wall's feed, so a tablet left on the bench can't show tasks, health, money or notes. **Make a new address** stops the old one; **Turn off** ends it.

New: `GET /api/v1/wall?key=` (no sign-in), and `GET`/`POST`/`DELETE /api/v1/wall/key` (signed in). The page is `/wall`.

## Undo instead of "Are you sure?"

These now happen at once, with **Undo** in a banner for six seconds:

- deleting a saved workout or a routine;
- removing an exercise or a set from a workout in progress;
- removing a goal, a renewal or a receipt;
- deleting a form video or one of your handbook commands;
- stopping a habit.

The change is only sent when the banner goes. Undo puts it back, including in a workout, where it goes back to its place with anything changed since kept. Leaving the app sends what's waiting straight away. If sending fails, the item comes back and the banner says so.

Still asked first: deleting a range of health data (that can't be undone), discarding a whole workout, and settings. Deleting a Siris note already had its own undo.

## A fuller monthly recap

"Last month in short" (on Home from the 1st, filed in Siris) now also has:

- **the gym:** workouts against the month before, kilos lifted, and new bests;
- **goals** behind the pace for the time of year;
- **where you went:** the sections opened most (from the visit counts that began in 11.10);
- **renewals due** in the next 30 days.

## A privacy check on every change

The repo is public. CI now has a **privacy** check (`scripts/privacy_check.py`) that reads only the lines a pull request adds, and fails on:

- a LAN address, unless the line says `privacy: ok`;
- an email address other than example ones;
- anything on your own private list: names, a street, real figures.

The private list is a GitHub Actions secret, `PRIVACY_TERMS` (comma-separated), so it's never in the repo itself. A number matches with or without thousands commas. A failure names the file and line, never the private value. Run it before a commit with `python3 scripts/privacy_check.py --staged`.
