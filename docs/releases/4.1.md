# SirisOS 4.1

- **Your year**, in the menu: the last 12 months, month by month, with a small chart for each of these:
  - **CPD hours**, with your three-year Engineers Australia total against 150
  - **running** kilometres
  - **tasks done**, from any list, wherever you ticked them off
  - **journal days** from the wrap-up, with your current streak and your longest
  - **the Inbox at its busiest**, as the average of each day's high
- Tap or hover a month to see its number. **Table** shows the whole year as a table.
- SirisOS now keeps a small daily log, so tasks done, the Inbox and journal days build up from today. Before today it has the last 60 days of tasks and wrap-ups.

## Deployment

A full year of running needs SirisAI 2.26 or later; older versions show the last 35 days.
