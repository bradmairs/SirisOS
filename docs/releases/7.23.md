# SirisOS 7.23

## Health → Data sync

Apple Health's sync ("Apple Health, on its own") has moved from Training to a new **Data sync** tab under Health. It has the address and key for Health Auto Export, what the phone sent lately, the setup check, and **Test the sync**. Training points there, and Health's "See what arrived" and "Set up Apple Health" links go there too.

## Terminal

The terminal no longer hides its last line (where you type) while you're typing in it.

- **The cause:** xterm, the terminal SirisOS uses, puts the classes `terminal` and, while you type in it, `focus` on its own element. SirisOS used both names for its own styles: `.focus` is Home's Focus card, with a 16 px top margin, and `.terminal` is the session's layout. So the moment you clicked in, the terminal's text moved 16 px down and its last line went past the bottom of the box. Clicking away removed `focus`, which is why it looked right again. SirisOS's own classes are now `focus-card` and `terminal-session`, and a test stops xterm's class names being styled again.
- **Also:** the box can't be scrolled by the browser any more (it used to scroll to show xterm's hidden typing field). Its 8 px padding moved onto the terminal itself, where its fit allows for it. On a phone it's sized to what's visible: from its top to the keyboard or tab bar, less the keys under it (iOS doesn't take the keyboard out of the screen height). It brings itself into view when the keyboard comes up and keeps the cursor line in view.
- Checked in Chromium on 7.22 (12 px of the terminal hidden, shifting when you click in) and on this version at 1024×700, 1280×800, 1440×900, 1920×1080 and phone size: the typing line stays inside the box before and while typing.
