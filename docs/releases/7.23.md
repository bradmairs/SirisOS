# SirisOS 7.23

## Health → Data sync

Apple Health's sync ("Apple Health, on its own") has moved from Training to a new **Data sync** tab under Health. It has the address and key for Health Auto Export, what the phone sent lately, the setup check, and **Test the sync**. Training points there, and Health's "See what arrived" and "Set up Apple Health" links go there too.

## Terminal

The terminal no longer hides its last line, where you type. Its height was a fixed guess (the screen less the tab bar less 340 px). That guess ignored the iPhone keyboard (iOS doesn't take it out of the screen height), the rows of keys under the terminal, and how far the page had scrolled, so the bottom rows ended up behind the keyboard or tab bar. It now sizes itself to what's actually visible, from its top to the keyboard or tab bar, less the keys under it. It follows the keyboard opening and closing, brings the terminal into view when the keyboard comes up, and keeps the cursor line in view. The 8 px inner padding also moved onto the terminal itself, where the fit addon allows for it.
