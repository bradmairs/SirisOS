# SirisOS 6.62

Everything in SI units.

- **SirisGYM is kilograms only.** The kg/lb setting is gone. A gym kept in pounds is converted to kilograms when it next loads: every logged weight, the bar, the plates and the increment. Imports from apps that log in pounds (a header that says lb, or choosing "lb (converted to kg)") are saved in kilograms.
- **Energy in kilojoules.** Active energy on Health → Today and each workout's energy are shown in kJ instead of kcal. Apple Health still sends kcal, and it's kept that way underneath.
- With SirisAI 4.05: spoken and chat health summaries, goals and meal notes use kJ, and a car range reported in miles is shown in km.

Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder.
