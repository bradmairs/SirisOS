# SirisOS 6.9

SirisGYM, round three: a programme builder, your body, the lock screen, your voice, and SirisAI as a coach.

- **Build me a programme** (Routines). Describe a goal ("4 days, hypertrophy, bad left shoulder, an hour in the gym") or pick the days, goal, equipment, minutes and any area to go easy on (shoulder, elbow, wrist, lower back, knee). It drafts a split into the routine editor: full body for 2-3 days, upper/lower for 4, upper/lower/push/pull/legs for 5, and push/pull/legs twice for 6. Each day is filled with exercises that suit your equipment and spare the injured area, with sets, reps and rest for the goal, and trimmed to fit your time. Change anything, then save.
- **Body** (Progress). Measurements in cm (waist, chest, hips, arm, thigh, calf, neck) with the change since your first, next to your weight trend from Apple Health.
- **Progress photos** (Progress). Front, side or back, kept only on your server. Two dates side by side to compare.
- **Rest's over, on the lock screen.** When a rest starts, SirisOS sends a push when it ends, with what's next ("Bench Press, set 3: 82.5 kg"), so you can lock the phone between sets. Turn on "SirisGYM" in your notification settings. A device that already had every topic turned on gets it automatically.
- **Log a set by voice.** Tap the mic in a workout and say "100 by 5", "bench 82.5 kilos for 8 RPE 8", "5 reps at 60" or "bodyweight 12". It fills the next set, ticks it and starts the rest.
- **Ask SirisAI** (with SirisAI 4.16). After each workout SirisOS sends SirisAI a summary, so chat and voice can answer "how's my bench trending?", "what should I do in the gym today?" and "am I training legs enough?". It's health data, so it stays off cloud models when health privacy is on.
- **The weekly review** has a gym section: workouts, sets, tonnes lifted, records, muscles under target, and whether a lighter week is due.

Needs SirisAI 4.16. Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder.
