# SirisOS 11.10.0

**Edit habits, working hours for each day, a Server handbook, and a Home that learns where you go.**

## Habits you can edit

On Health → Habits, the pencil beside a habit opens it:

- rename it;
- change its days;
- move it **Up** or **Down** the list;
- **Stop tracking** it. Its history is kept, and **Stopped habits** lists the ones you stopped, to **Bring back** with their days.

New: `PATCH /api/v1/mind/habits/{id}` takes `move` (-1 or 1) and `archived: false`, and the habit list includes `stopped`.

## Working hours for each day

The pencil beside the hours on Work's day card sets a start and finish for each day, or a day off:

- the ring, "home time" and the countdown follow that day's hours; a day off says so;
- the day plan puts work tasks in that day's hours;
- **Use Times of day instead** goes back to one set of hours, Monday to Friday, as before.

New: `hours_by_day` in `PUT /api/v1/work/settings` (Monday first; null for a day off).

## A handbook for the server

Server has a **Handbook** tab: 68 Linux, Docker, GPU, network and NAS commands, with the dangerous ones flagged.

- Search across them, and copy any one with a tap.
- **Fixes that worked before** has the commands that fixed this server's own problems: the Obsidian relay, Hermes, and a model only partly on the GPU.
- **Add your own**: they're kept on the server, not in the code, so your own hosts and paths stay private.

New: `/api/v1/handbook` (GET, POST, PATCH, DELETE).

## Home learns where you go

- **Usually now.** SirisOS counts the sections you open at each part of the day. Ones you've opened on three or more days in the last four weeks at this time are at the top of Home, one tap away: SirisGym in the evening, Brief in the morning. The counts also show which sections go unused.
- **Needs you** puts SirisAI's notes about itself (a self-test answer it got wrong, a check after an update) on one line at the end, with **Got it, all of them**. They had filled Home's top every morning. An urgent one is never folded away, and the full inbox is unchanged.
- **On time.** Coming back to the app after a while, Home asks for the part of the day at once, instead of at its next five-minute refresh.
- Loading placeholders were being counted as cards; they aren't now, and the ones counted before are cleared.

## Fixes

- **The backup copy** (`SIRISOS_BACKUP_COPY_PATH`) is refused when it's the backup folder itself, and only ever clears its own nightly copies. On the server, the backups were already on the NAS and the copy pointed at the same folder, so its clean-up removed the 30 October nightly. The setting has been off on the server since 13 November.
- `scripts/bump-version.sh` writes the README's version line with perl: macOS's `sed -i -E` took `-E` as a backup suffix and stopped the script.

- **`make update`** now exists: it pulls main, then rebuilds and starts SirisOS. Every release's notes have named it, but the Makefile never had it.

## Update

`make update` on the server, then open SirisOS once so the new version loads.
