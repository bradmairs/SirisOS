# SirisOS 3.5

- **Weekly review** (in the menu). A notification offers it on Sunday afternoon, and it has four parts:
  1. **The week in numbers:** tasks done, notes added to Siris (against the week before), CPD hours, runs and kilometres, exercise minutes, meetings, and days you wrapped up. Below that, a list of what you got done.
  2. **Going quiet:** Siris projects nobody has touched in three weeks.
  3. **Next week:** how many meetings, busy days (four or more meetings, or clashes), tasks due and project deadlines, with a link to next week's view.
  4. **What matters next week:** one note, saved to Siris (tagged `weekly-review`) and shown at the top of **This week** all next week.
- Change when the notification comes with `SIRISOS_REVIEW_WEEKDAY` (6 = Sunday) and `SIRISOS_REVIEW_HOUR` (16).
- Runs come from the iPhone Health shortcut's new `running_km` (SirisAI 2.14). Without it, the runs line is simply left out.
