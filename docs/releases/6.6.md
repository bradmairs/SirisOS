# SirisOS 6.6

The gym is now **SirisGYM**, and it can bring in years of workouts from JEFIT (or any app that exports CSV).

- **Import from JEFIT (or any CSV)**, in SirisGYM → History. Choose the export (a .csv, or a .zip of them) and you see what it found first: how many workouts and sets, the date range, and every exercise name with what it matches in SirisGYM (same name, same exercise, best guess, or no match). Change any match, or make it a new exercise of your own, then import.
- Reads JEFIT's export (its exercise log table, with sets written like `60x10,62.5x8`) and other apps' row-per-set CSVs (date, exercise, weight, reps, and set, time or distance if they're there). Dates as 2024-03-05, 05/03/2024 (day first) or a timestamp.
- **Pounds or kilos**: weights are taken as lb when the file's header says so, or when you pick "lb", and saved in your SirisGYM unit.
- **Safe to run again**: each day becomes one workout (`import-YYYYMMDD`), so importing the same export again replaces those days instead of doubling them. The preview says how many it will replace. Imported workouts count towards records, history, charts and what to lift next.
- The section is called SirisGYM in the menu and page title.

API: `POST /api/v1/gym/import` (form `file`, `unit` auto/kg/lb, `dry_run` default true, `choices` JSON).

Update: `make update` in the `deploy` folder.
