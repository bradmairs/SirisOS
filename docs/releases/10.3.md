# SirisOS 10.3

- **Slide along the tab bar.** On a phone, the bubble under the tab you're on now slides from tab to tab, like iOS's Liquid Glass tab bar.
  - **Drag it:** put your finger on the bar and slide. The bubble follows, swells a little and turns clearer, and the tab under your finger lights up.
  - **Let go** over a tab to open it.
  - **A plain tap still works**, and tapping the tab you're on still scrolls back to the top.
- **Fold the spending lines away.** On Budget, the Spending card's arrow folds its lines away (to the month's total) and back. This device remembers it.
- **Your bank files, listed.** The Spending card lists each CSV you've added, with its account, how many transactions it brought and the dates it covers.
  - **Take out** a file you added by mistake: its transactions go, and anything you sorted from it.
  - **Add another CSV:** the button for the next file.
- **No duplicates.** The same purchase is now only counted once, even when two exports overlap or the same file was added under another account name. Two identical purchases in one file, like two coffees on the same day, still count as two. Duplicates already in from earlier are cleared out once, when you update.

To update: `make update`.
