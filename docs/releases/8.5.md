# SirisOS 8.5

Brad's SirisOS list:

- **Home Lab: a diagram of the hardware.** A new page, from System status's neighbour in the menu, drawn from what SirisOS already knows:
  - network gear (gateway, switches, access points) and devices marked important, from the network watch;
  - servers, from the terminal's machines;
  - Docker's containers, from SirisAI.

  What plugs into what is guessed (shown dashed) until you set it. Tap a box to add its model and notes, or move it. **Add hardware** covers what SirisOS can't see: a modem, a UPS, a NAS. On a phone it's an indented tree; on wider screens it's a top-down diagram. Your details are kept in `homelab.json` (`SIRISOS_HOMELAB_PATH`).
- **Training is now Fitness**, with two tabs. **Running** is the race plan, as before. **Activity** is training load, steps and workouts, moved from Health. Old links to `/training` and Health's Fitness tab come here. A tab-bar choice of Training becomes Fitness.
- **Drag a sheet down to close it** (SirisGym's exercise history, a workout, any sheet), as on iOS. Pull from the top: past about 110 px, or with a quick flick, it closes; otherwise it springs back.
- **When everyone's out:** Smart home's "Who counts as home" card now chooses what SirisAI does: **Nothing** (for when Apple Home's own automation already does it), **Ask me**, or **Run it**. It also says how long the phones must be gone: SirisAI 5.63 waits 10 minutes without a break, so a phone asleep off the Wi-Fi isn't anyone leaving.
- **No more "leave by" times.** They were the event's start less a fixed margin, not worked out from where you are. iOS's calendar travel-time alerts do it properly. The setting is gone from Times of day.
- **The OneNote handwriting check is gone** (OneNote is out of Siris). SirisAI 5.63 also stops the nightly OneNote import, which had kept running.
- **Wind-down on desktop, fixed:**
  - The "Today" card's title no longer squashes to "TODA / Y" beside the date. A card's name never gives way; plain text beside it does, and buttons size to their words.
  - A Home section with nothing to show (the evening's empty "Home") isn't drawn.
  - "Ask about this page" is in the sidebar, no longer floating over the cards.
- **One card can't blank the app.** A card whose data is missing a field now steps aside instead of taking the whole screen down.

Needs SirisAI 5.63 for the away-mode choice.
