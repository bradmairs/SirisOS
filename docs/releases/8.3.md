# SirisOS 8.3

- **SirisAI asks you to set your year's goals.** All through January, Home shows a card from SirisAI asking you to set the year's goals. It stays until you press **I've set them**, here or on Your Year, and one notification goes out at the start of the month.
- **And to review them in December.** All through December, the card asks you to review the year's goals. **Review them** opens Your Year, which shows how many were done. Anything unfinished is ticked to carry into next year (untick any you're letting go), then **Finish the review**. Carried goals start fresh next year, noted "Carried from <year>", and one with the same name already there isn't doubled. A December review you missed is asked for first thing in January.
- **Delete a note from the mind map.** Open a note in the Siris mind map and press **Delete note**. SirisOS asks first, the note goes to Siris' deleted notes, and **Undo** brings it back. Needs Siris engine 2.39.
- **Full-screen mind map clears the menu.** On desktop it now starts right of the SirisOS menu, so the map's search and filters aren't covered.
