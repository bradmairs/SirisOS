# SirisOS 6.7

SirisGYM, round one of the big upgrade: settings, gyms, warm-ups, supersets, effort and notes, progression rules, and a year at a glance.

- **Settings** (SirisGYM → Settings): rest between sets, the weight step, warm-ups on or off and their scheme (e.g. `40x5, 60x3, 80x2`), and your gyms.
- **Gyms.** Each gym has its bar, plates, the dumbbells on its rack and its machine stack step. Pick the gym on Today before you start. Suggested weights are rounded to what that gym can actually load (plates on the bar, the nearest dumbbell, the machine's step), and the plate calculator uses that gym's bar and plates. Each workout remembers where it was done.
- **Warm-ups worked out for you.** Starting a routine day adds warm-up sets before the first lift for each muscle: the empty bar for 10, then 40%, 60% and 80% of the working weight, each one loadable.
- **Supersets and circuits.** Group exercises in a routine (Superset A, B …), or link two in a workout with "Superset with the next". The rest timer runs after the last exercise in the group, not after each one.
- **Effort and notes.** Tap RPE on a finished set (6 easy to 10 nothing left), and add a note per exercise. Sets that hit the top and felt easy add twice the step next time; a grind holds the weight. Last time's note shows when you do the exercise again.
- **Progression rules** per routine exercise: top of the range then add (as before), add every session (two misses in a row backs off 10%), or a percentage of your training max (90% of your best estimated 1RM in 90 days), one share per set.
- **The last year** (History): a square a day, darker for more volume, with the sets and main muscles on each.

Update: `make update` in SirisAI's `deploy` folder.
