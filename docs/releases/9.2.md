# SirisOS 9.2

Mental health, and a new Habits tab:

- **Face ID for your journal.** With a passkey set up (Security), the journal's entries open only after Face ID, and SirisOS checks it like a sign-in. They lock again after five minutes without use, when you leave the app, or with **Lock**. You can still write while it's locked. Nothing behind the lock is cached on the phone.
- **More in "What helps".** As well as sleep, exercise, mindful minutes and daylight, your mood is now compared:
  - the day after 3 drinks or more against fewer;
  - on days with 300 mg of caffeine or more against less;
  - on days you trained at the gym against days you didn't.
- **Mood comes from Apple Health only.** The quick mood buttons are gone from Mental health. Log mood in the Health app (State of Mind) or the Watch's Mindfulness app, and it arrives through Health Auto Export.
- **Habits**, a new Health tab. Daily habits each show a streak, your best, how often in the last 30 days and a 30-day grid. Tap a day in the grid to fix a missed tick. Stopping a habit keeps its history.
