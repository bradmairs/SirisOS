# SirisOS 8.1

- **Receipts are kept, not only what they bought.** The Receipts card in Life admin lists each receipt with its shop, date and total. Open one to see the email as it came, and any PDF or photo attached. It's searchable.
- **Things link to their receipt.** A Receipt button on each thing opens it. Things already added from email get linked to theirs as those emails are read again.
- **Your Receipts folder counts in full.** Everything in it is read and kept, whatever its subject or date. The rest of your mail, except Spam and Trash, is searched for subjects that sound like receipts.
- **Safe to open.** The email is shown in a sandbox: nothing in it can run, and pictures from the web don't load, so senders can't tell you opened it.
- Removing a receipt leaves the thing, and the email stays in your mailbox. It won't be kept here again.
- Letters and bills no longer lists receipts: they have their own card.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 5.31.

```bash
git pull && make up
```
