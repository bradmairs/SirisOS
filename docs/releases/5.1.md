# SirisOS 5.1

**From your notes: one older note a day.**

A new Home card, **From your notes**, brings back one older Siris note a day that's related to what you're doing now. It looks at your personal projects, your open tasks, and your calendar for today and tomorrow, then finds the note that best matches them.

- A note qualifies when it was last updated at least a month ago and hasn't been shown in the last 90 days.
- Work notes, daily notes, dashboards, templates and the Inbox are left out, and so are the projects themselves.
- The card says why it picked the note ("Related to your project Home Lab") and how old it is.
- **Read it** opens the note in Siris. **Seen it** closes the card for today. **Not useful** means you'll never see that note again, and the card brings another.
- It shows in the morning, at weekends and while winding down. Change that in Home → Times of day.

## Deployment

```bash
git pull && make up
```
