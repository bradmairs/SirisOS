# SirisOS 8.8

SirisGym: the next round, built for the iPhone and checked at iPhone size on a copy of Brad's real data.

## In the gym
- **The screen stays on** during a workout (Settings → Keep the screen on). iOS lets go when the app's hidden, so it's asked for again when you come back.
- **Log a set with Siri.** Settings → Log sets with Siri makes a key (shown once) for an iPhone Shortcut: "Hey Siri, log a set", "100 by 5". Siri says "Got it: 100 by 5". The set waits on SirisOS and goes into the exercise you're on, with the rest timer, within a few seconds of SirisGym being open. The key can only add sets to that queue; a new key or **Turn off** stops the old one.
- **How's today going?** Under today's workout: **As planned**, **I'm tired** (a set fewer and 5% lighter, never heavier than last time) or **Short on time** (the first 60% of the exercises, at least 2).
- **More set types.** Tap a set's number: warm-up (W), drop (D), as many reps as possible (A), rest-pause (R).
- **Form cues** under each exercise: built in for the big lifts, or your own (an exercise's sheet → Cues → Edit).
- **Swaps for a gym.** Swap an exercise with **Every time at <gym>** ticked, and that gym gets the swap from then on.

## Planning
- **Planned lighter weeks.** A routine can have a lighter week every 4, 5, 6 or 8 weeks, counted from the block's start. That week is a deload on its own, and the "time for a lighter week?" nag stops.
- **The next block.** A routine runs for a number of weeks (6 unless you change it). Then Today shows how each lift moved: keep what's moving, swap a stalled lift for another one for the same muscles that you have history with, or change its reps. You untick anything you'd rather keep, and **Start the next block** saves a copy of your gym file first.

## Progress
- **Muscles over time** (Progress): working sets a month for each muscle over 1 or 2 years, with the last 3 full months against the 3 before.
- **Bodyweight counts in volume.** Pull-ups, dips and push-ups count your bodyweight (from Apple Health, or the session's), plus any weight added.
- **Monthly measure-up reminder.** On the 1st of each month at 9 am, a push to take measurements and progress photos.

## Housekeeping
- **Old gym backups clear themselves.** The last 10 copies, and every copy from the past 30 days, are kept. Older ones go.
