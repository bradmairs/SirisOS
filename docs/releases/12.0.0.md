# SirisOS 12.0.0

**SirisTrails: your walks and hikes on a map.**

A new section under Health (Health → SirisTrails) keeps **every hike** and **every outdoor walk of an hour or more** from Apple Health, each with its GPS route. Shorter walks, indoor walks and other workouts stay where they were.

## What it shows

- **One map of everything**, with topographic, street and satellite views. Hikes are orange and walks blue; tap a line to open that walk.
- **Totals** for all time or a chosen year: how many walks, distance, time on foot, metres climbed, the highest point and the longest walk.
- **Each walk:**
  - its route, with start and finish marked
  - distance, time and moving time, and pace
  - ascent and descent (smoothed, so GPS wobble doesn't count as climbing) and the highest point
  - heart rate and energy, when Health has them
  - an elevation profile
- **Your own name and notes** for each walk. Delete one, with Undo, and a later import won't bring it back.

## Bringing walks in

- **Everything so far:** in the Health app, tap your picture → **Export All Health Data**, and upload the export.zip on SirisTrails. Every qualifying walk with a recorded route comes in.
- **New walks as they happen:** in Health Auto Export, add an automation for **Workouts** with **Include Route Data** on, posting to the same address and key as your health data. SirisOS keeps the walks and passes the rest to SirisAI as before, now without the route points, which it doesn't use.
- The same walk from both sources, or from uploading twice, is kept once.

Where you walk stays on your server: no place names are looked up. Only the map tiles come from the internet (OpenTopoMap, OpenStreetMap, Esri).

New: `/api/v1/trails` (list, one walk, rename and notes, delete, Apple Health export upload and its status).
