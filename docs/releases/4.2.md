# SirisOS 4.2

- **Home controls on Home.** A card with your lights, garage door, locks and scenes, one tap each:
  - **Lights** switch on or off straight away and show their brightness.
  - **The garage door** asks "Open Garage door?" first, every time.
  - **Locks** lock with one tap. Unlocking asks first.
  - **Scenes** from Siris and Home Assistant run with one tap.
- In guest mode the garage and locks aren't shown and can't be used.
- SirisAI records every tap in its audit log. You can move or hide the card under **Customise**, like any other.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 2.46 or later. To choose which lights and doors show, and in what order, set `SIRISAI_HUB_CONTROLS` in SirisAI's `.env`, e.g. `light.kitchen,light.deck,cover.garage_door,lock.front_door`. By default it shows every light, garage door and lock.
