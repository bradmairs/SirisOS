# SirisOS 6.2

**A home that knows its things: device inventory, energy by device, and upkeep by how much things run.** Needs SirisAI 3.83.

- **Device inventory.** On Smart home → Network, tap the pencil on any device to give it a name, an owner and a type. Its room comes from where it sits in the 3D house, and your name labels it in the house too. Alerts now read "Lounge TV (Lounge) is offline" instead of a MAC address.
- **Fewer new-device alerts.** A device UniFi has a name for, or first saw over a day ago, is never "new". When SirisAI is already watching for new devices (with Name it and Block in the Inbox), SirisOS doesn't send a second notification about the same one.
- **Energy by device.** The Power card lists today's biggest users, with each one's kWh and share of the day. It also gives the house's **always-on** draw: the lowest it got between 1am and 5am, what that adds up to over a year, and which sensors never drop below 15 W. Set `SIRISOS_POWER_TARIFF` (dollars per kWh) to see the yearly cost too.
- **Maintenance by use.** A job can come due by how much something has run, as well as (or instead of) by date. Point it at a Home Assistant power sensor ("every 40 hours of use", counted while it draws over 20 W) or an energy meter ("every 1,500 kWh"). Whichever comes first wins. Marking it done starts the count again.

## Deployment

On the server, in SirisAI's `deploy` folder:

```bash
make update
```
