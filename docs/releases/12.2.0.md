# SirisOS 12.2.0

**SirisTrails: where you've walked, records, and a tidier page.**

## More numbers

- **Totals** now include the average walk (distance and time), average pace, and how many countries, states or regions, and towns or suburbs you've walked in.
- **Where you've walked:** each country with its flag, walks, distance and hours. Tap a country to see its states or regions, each with walks, kilometres and places, and to zoom the map there. **Whole map** goes back.
- **Records:** longest walk, longest time, most climbing and highest walk. Tap one to open it.
- **By year:** distance and walks for each year as bars. Tap a year to see just that year.

## Fixes

- The totals no longer run into each other on a phone: they're tiles that wrap.
- The map no longer shows grey bands above and below the world when zoomed out, and it's shorter on a phone.
- The year buttons are one row that scrolls sideways, instead of wrapping onto two lines.

## Names, faster

The public OpenStreetMap track lookup (Overpass) is often overloaded, which left most walks waiting. Naming now has two passes:
- **Fast pass:** every walk gets its place, state and country, and a hike its peak, from Nominatim in about a second each.
- **Slow pass:** named tracks and parks come from Overpass a few walks at a time, as it allows.

A walk you've renamed keeps your name.
