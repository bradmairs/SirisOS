# SirisOS 9.23.0

_Was 9.9 before the 2026-11-22 renumbering (a feature)._

- **Friday debrief on Home** (with SirisAI 4.18.0). From Friday until you answer, a card on Home asks about the work week:
  - **What you decided and why, what went wrong, what you'd do differently.** Answer any of them, and pick the project it's about (or none).
  - **The week's CPEng question**, about an element with no evidence yet, when SirisAI asked one.
  - **Where it goes:** Siris files each answer on the project's note (or Work Debriefs). The decision also goes in the Decision Log, what you'd do differently in Lessons Learned, and the CPEng example where the nightly evidence check reads it.
  - **Hidden** when there's no debrief open, or when SirisAI is older than 7.07.
- Backend: `GET` and `POST /api/v1/brain/debrief` pass through to SirisAI's `/siris/brain/debrief`.
