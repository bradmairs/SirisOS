# SirisOS 11.3.0

**SirisOS does more of the thinking.**

- **Quick capture files things itself.** Type or say one line, and it goes where it belongs:
  - "Spent $42 at Bunnings" goes on the budget, sorted onto a line. When the bank's CSV arrives with the same spend, the bank's line replaces it.
  - "Tacos Thursday" or "dinner tomorrow: spag bol" goes in Meals.
  - "Bench felt heavy today" goes on today's workout in SirisGym (never in Siris).
  - "Call Sam back Friday" becomes a task due then.

  It always says where it put the line, with an **Undo**. Anything else goes to Siris as a note, as before.
- **A plan for the day.** A Home card fits today's tasks (and anything overdue) into the free time in your calendar, until wind-down.
  - **When things go:** focus work goes in the longest gap, early when readiness is good. Quick calls and emails slot in around it, and errands go in one run. On a weekday, work tasks stay in work hours.
  - **On a low-readiness day,** it plans one focus block at most.
  - **It keeps up:** it's worked out again every time you look, so a meeting that runs over or a task done early just reshuffles the rest.
- **Home learns what you use.** Cards you tap move up their section. A card with something to tap that you haven't touched on 10 of the last 28 days is tucked under **Show everything**, and tapping it there brings it back. Cards there just to read (the weather, the clock) are never tucked. Turn it off in Customise Home.
- **Bills on the timeline.** Repeating charges found in your bank files now show in Today's **Coming up** for the three days before they're due.
- **Price rises:** you get a notification once when a repeating charge goes up ("Netflix went up $4.00").
- **Patterns in your days.** A Home card compares your own numbers over the last four months:
  - **What it compares:** drinks against next morning's HRV; coffee, busy days, evening workouts and steps against sleep; busy days and short nights against spending; HRV against how much you lifted.
  - **When it speaks:** only when a difference is clear and holds in both halves of the period. It says it in plain words, with one thing to try.
  - **Notifications:** a new pattern sends one on a Sunday morning.
- **Ask SirisAI about SirisOS.** With SirisAI 4.25.0, it can answer from SirisOS's own data:
  - "What did I spend on fuel last month?"
  - "When did we last have spag bol?"
  - "How far behind am I on my CPD goal?"
  - "What's left in the groceries budget?"
  - "What's my plan today?"

  It reads only, through its service key.
- **Goals that steer themselves.** Your year shows each goal's pace ("on pace for 1,008 of 1,200 km").
  - **When one slips,** you get a small step for this week, sized to catch up ("run 50 km this week: 4 runs of about 12 km"). It goes in a free gap in the day's plan, a Home card shows it, and you get a Monday-evening notification.
- **While you were away.** After 20 hours or more without SirisOS open, Home starts with what changed and what's waiting, most important first:
  - what needs you
  - tasks
  - money
  - goals
  - the next 24 hours
  - what's in the roundup

  **Got it** marks it all seen, so those notifications don't come again.

To update: `make update` (and update SirisAI to 4.25.0 for the questions).
