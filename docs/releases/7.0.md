# SirisOS 7.0

The fixes and additions from a full live test of SirisOS.

## SirisGym

- **Imports keep every set.** The 40-set limit is gone: an export now brings in everything it has. To get back sets cut by the old limit, upload your JEFIT export again.
- **Days that can't be one workout get checked.** An imported day with more than 60 working sets or over 40 tonnes is held back from records, streaks, the year in review and the coach. Today lists them: tap **It happened** to count one, or **Leave out** to keep it out. Nothing is deleted, and importing again keeps your decision.
- **Imported workouts have real names**, from what was trained: "Chest and Back", not "Imported workout". Old ones are renamed once, after a copy of your gym file is saved.
- **Merge duplicate exercises.** Exercises shows pairs that look the same, such as "Cable V Bar Pull Down" and "Cable V Bar Pulldown". Merging moves every set across and updates your routines, after saving a copy. Pairs with different equipment, like barbell and dumbbell, aren't offered.
- **The Last column lines up.** It compares working sets with working sets, so warm-ups no longer push it out of step.
- **Finish warns about sets you haven't ticked**, since they aren't saved.
- **More room in a workout.** The tab bar hides while you train, and each set starts with last time's reps at that weight, or the bottom of the rep range.
- **A stall means a recent stall.** The deload suggestion compares your last three sessions of a lift with the six before, and skips lifts you've stopped doing. It used to compare with your all-time best, which called most lifts stalled for good.
- **Effort advice.** A workout linked to your Watch now ends with plain advice for next time.
- **Morning gym card.** From 6 to 8 am, Home shows today's workout and readiness, with one tap to start it. If you've set a start Shortcut in SirisGym's settings, that tap runs it too, so it can start the Watch.
- **Weekly coach.** One or two changes for the week from your training, sleep, recovery and protein, on SirisGym's Today and in the weekly review. Needs SirisAI 4.29.

## Speed

- The Siris tab's dot uses a light count (0.16 s) instead of working out all the insights.
- Gym pages no longer wait about 2 seconds for health data each time: it's kept for five minutes and refreshed behind the scenes.
- The weekly review and the brief show what they last had straight away, then refresh.
- Every screen now names its browser tab.

## Looking after itself

- **SirisAI going quiet.** If SirisAI doesn't answer for three minutes, even with its container up, one alert goes to the Inbox and your phone.
- **Daily self-test.** The live checks behind `make verify` run each morning from 5 am. Anything broken goes to the Inbox and your phone. Things worth a look go to the Inbox quietly, and a dismissal holds until that list changes. The result is shown on the Status page.
- **Test mode.** With `SIRISOS_TEST_MODE=1`, a copy of SirisOS can read live services but sends them nothing: no workouts, chat, actions or notifications. Home shows a banner, and the background checks stay off. Features that need to send something, including chat, won't work in this mode.

## Deployment

Needs SirisAI 4.29 and Siris engine 1.88 for the coach, the light count and fast insights.

```bash
git pull && make up
```

The first start renames old imported workouts, keeping `gym.before-titles.<time>.json` beside your gym file.
