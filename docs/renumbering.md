# SirisOS renumbering (2026-11-22)

Brad's rule: a whole-number change is a significant new section (like Health or SirisGym), +0.1 a new feature or a bigger
upgrade, +0.01 a fix or minor change. Decimals rolled over (1.9 + 0.1 = 2.0), so versions are now written
**Section.Feature.Fix** and each part counts on its own. Every release was judged a section, a feature or a fix and the
history counted again from 1.0.0, in the order the releases shipped. GitHub's tags and releases were recreated under the
new numbers on the same commits.

The new sections: Tasks (2.0.0), Smart home (3.0.0), Training (4.0.0), Health (5.0.0), SirisGym (6.0.0), Work (7.0.0),
Life admin (8.0.0), Budget, things and people (9.0.0), Meals and trips (10.0.0) and Around the week (11.0.0).
